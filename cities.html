San Diego, CA

Crime & Justice

San Diego County District Attorney Summer Stephan's office announced on September 4 that it will not file criminal charges against SDPD Officer Daniel Gold, who fatally shot 16-year-old Konoa Wilson twice in the back on the night of January 28 as the teen fled gunfire from someone else. The decision comes after the City Council quietly settled the family's wrongful-death lawsuit for $30 million in a December closed session, one of the largest police payouts in the city's history. Now a separate fight has opened over transparency: on August 19, First Amendment Coalition legal director David Loy sent a letter demanding the city release body-camera and other critical-incident recordings required under California public-records law, and the city responded that the footage remains exempt while the (now-closed) investigation and confidentiality concerns are cited. The coalition has signaled it may sue if the records are not released, meaning the case is likely to keep generating headlines well after the DA's decision closes the criminal side of the story.

Government & Public Safety

The city has launched a multi-department push to clean up downtown's Gaslamp Quarter after months of complaints about late-night disorder. Since August 28, "Operation Last Call" has added visible and undercover police patrols on Friday and Saturday nights, and the department credits the surge with 76 arrests, 102 citations and five firearms recovered so far. Public works crews have added a temporary Saturday-morning street-sweeping shift for six weeks and are repainting curbs and crosswalks and replacing damaged signage along 4th, 5th and 6th avenues between Broadway and L Street. The city also created dedicated rideshare pickup zones on 5th Avenue this summer to cut down on curbside congestion and pedestrian conflicts. Officials are coordinating the effort with the Downtown San Diego Partnership and Gaslamp businesses, who have pushed for a visible response as the district tries to protect its tourism and nightlife economy heading into the fall convention season.
