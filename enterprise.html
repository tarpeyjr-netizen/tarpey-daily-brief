Enterprise Software Intel — Week of November 12, 2026

🏢 Platform News

S/4HANA

The clock is still ticking: mainstream maintenance for ECC EHP 6–8 ends December 31, 2027, with optional extended maintenance to the end of 2030 at a premium. ECC keeps running after that, but SAP support changes. Adoption estimates still diverge widely. One advisory firm cites Gartner as saying only 39% of SAP’s roughly 35,000 ECC customers have licensed S/4HANA. That source sells third-party support, so weigh it accordingly (Redress Compliance).

Joule

TechEd Berlin (Oct 27–29) put Joule Studio at the center. It offers no-code and pro-code agent building across 70+ models, with new views of agent behavior, model usage and spend. Custom agents run free on SAP’s managed runtime through December 2026 under a time-limited promotion. Joule Work adds A2A/MCP support and agentic planning, and is in phased GA with about 200 early-adopter customers (SAP News; ERP Today). Analysts note that SAP gave no method behind its claim of 20% productivity gains from its internal rollout (FourWeekMBA).

SAP Analytics Cloud

No news this week. The quarterly release highlights are the cadence to follow (SAP release highlights).

SAP Integration Suite

Nothing new in November. To track Edge Integration Cell, AI features and PI/PO migration timing, watch the SAP user-group monthly update decks; the latest one seen is from July (SAP user groups).

SAP Build

No standalone news. Build’s story is now Joule Studio, which sits inside SAP Build, so the TechEd agent tooling above is the update to read.

WalkMe

SuccessFactors Learning now integrates with WalkMe Learning Arc. In Q4, authors can push AI-generated courses into SuccessFactors in one click (Futurum). Watch what happens to Enable Now: SAP’s messaging on its future has become vague (SAVIC).

Signavio

No 2026 news surfaced. Watch this year’s Transformation Excellence Summit, which typically falls in November, where last year’s Signavio features debuted (SAP News, 2025).

LeanIX

BTP

TechEd previewed a premium Enterprise AI Gateway that centralizes authentication, policy, routing and audit for AI traffic. It is planned for Q1 2027 and has no published price. Business Data Cloud gets a Dremio-based Iceberg lakehouse (GA planned Q1 2027), deeper SAP Databricks integration, Reltio MDM, an Ontology Manager beta and an EU AI Act governance assistant (ERP Today).

Salesforce

On Nov 5, Salesforce said Agentforce Coworker usage grew 21x in three months to more than 185,000 users, and that Adecco is expanding its rollout across 40 countries. Pricing lands at US$50 per user per month. Due this month: Teams integration, iOS and desktop apps, an MCP server and a free trial for Flex Credits customers. Due in December: Daily Digest and scheduled tasks (Salesforce; SalesforceBen).

ServiceNow

Q3 revenue of about $4.11B beat the top of guidance, and the 2026 AI ACV target rose to $1.7B. Shares still reportedly fell about 18% after a mixed print, and guidance flagged AI deployment investment trimming Q4 subscription growth by about 50bp (SiliconANGLE; Earnings Whispers).

Workday

FY27 Q3 results are due Nov 24 (Workday). The Rising follow-through continues. Workday is acquiring C1.ai (formerly ConductorOne) to govern identities for people and AI agents, with closing expected by Jan 31, 2027. Sana is now the default Workday Home page, and Workday says more than 5,500 customers use at least one of its 17 live agents (iTWire).

Microsoft 365

Ignite runs next week, Nov 17–20 in San Francisco, with the keynote on Nov 17. Expect it to set the Copilot agenda. Microsoft’s tentative November rollout list includes Autopilot to Frontier, Skills in Copilot Chat, a Workforce Insights Agent, Excel accounting skills and worldwide Home/Workspaces. Purview DLP for Cowork entered public preview in October (Microsoft Tech Community).

Databricks

Databricks is riding an August $5B round at a $190B valuation and a revenue run-rate above $7B (AI Magazine). It is now embedded deeper in SAP Business Data Cloud through Lakeflow, Agent Bricks and Lakebase. On the ecosystem side, CDW is buying Databricks partner Lovelytics for about $525M (Solutions Review). Housekeeping: the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash endpoint retires Nov 19 (release notes).
