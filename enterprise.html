🤖 AI in the Enterprise

  • Gartner forecasts 40% of enterprise apps will embed task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up from under 5% in 2025 (PYMNTS, 2026).
  • 97% of executives say their company deployed AI agents in the past year, but only about 31% run one in production — most stall at pilot (WRITER, 2026).
  • Enterprises abandoning AI initiatives jumped from 17% to 42% in a year, and the average org scraps 46% of proofs-of-concept before production (BeamSec, 2026).
  • SAP reversed its cloud-only stance, bringing Joule Work to on-prem ECC/S4 customers, with a Microsoft 365 Copilot ↔ SAP Joule agent-to-agent link planned (SAVIC Technologies, 2026).
  • Hype vs. reality: only 21% of organizations have mature governance for autonomous agents and 52% cite data quality as the top blocker — deployment headlines like Adecco's 27,000-seat Agentforce rollout read as momentum, but governance and data readiness, not model capability, are what's actually gating value.
