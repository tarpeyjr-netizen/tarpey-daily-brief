🤖 AI in the Enterprise

  • Hype vs. reality: Gartner's 2026 CIO survey finds only 17% of organizations have deployed AI agents, though more than 60% expect to within two years (Gartner). Its forecast that over 40% of agentic projects will be canceled by end-2027 still stands (Gartner). Every vendor here says "autonomous"; until a feature is GA under your license, assume a gap like Ignite's, where 65% of announced features were not GA.
  • Salesforce's August survey of agentic AI buyers says deployers reached ROI in about eight months. Teams that unified their data first got there faster, but only 31% did so before launch (Salesforce). (Vendor research.)
  • Agents are moving from chat windows to always-on background work: Databricks' Genie, Microsoft's Frontier-only Autopilot and SAP's Joule Work. Governance lags: most Agent 365 controls are still preview (Office Watch).
  • AI is moving to consumption pricing. SAP's CEO says the company will bill for AI consumption (Bloomberg), and Microsoft's add-ons include pay-per-use Copilot Credits. Budget for usage, not just seats.

⚠️ Threats & Risks

  • SAP's Nov 10 Patch Day fixed four CVSS 10.0 flaws that need no authentication and affect NetWeaver AS ABAP, Web Dispatcher and Gateway (ERP Today, The Hacker News). Reports disagree on the total number of notes. Patch internet-facing systems first. Some fixes need configuration changes too (SecurityOnline).
  • ServiceNow patched three maximum-severity flaws in its AI platform earlier this year (CSO). The date is unconfirmed and not this week, but check your patch level.
  • SaaS integration tokens remain the soft spot. The 2025 Salesloft Drift and Gainsight campaigns stole Salesforce data through OAuth tokens held by third-party apps (The Register). Audit connected apps before rolling out Coworker MCP or Copilot Salesforce plugins.
  • Lock-in: Forrester says SAP is trying to become the gatekeeper of enterprise AI by restricting third-party agents (Forrester). About half of DSAG members see a growing lock-in risk (E3).
  • Leadership churn: the Saueressig exit leaves SAP's customer-success organization in interim hands during RISE renewals (Constellation).
