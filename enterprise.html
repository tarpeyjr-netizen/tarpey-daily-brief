⚠️ Threats & Risks

  • SAP’s October patch day fixed 20 flaws, including CVE-2026-89111, a maximum-severity OAuth2 SSRF in the SAP Cloud SDK for Python that lets unauthenticated attackers steal client credentials; an allowlist for tenant_subdomain is the interim mitigation (BleepingComputer).
  • Also fixed: a critical access-control flaw in NetWeaver Visual Composer and a critical flaw in SAP Manufacturing Integration and Intelligence (SC Media).
  • The September “OVERPASS” kernel flaw (CVE-2026-44756, CVSS 10.0) is estimated to expose over 10,000 internet-facing SAP systems (Singapore CSA).
