🤖 AI in the Enterprise

  • Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic committed roughly $8B combined to enterprise AI deployment efforts in 2026; Microsoft launched a new "Frontier Company" business backed by $2.5B to focus specifically on making enterprise AI deployments actually succeed (TechCrunch).
  • 97% of executives say their company deployed AI agents in the past year, and Gartner forecasts 40% of enterprise applications will embed task-specific agents by year-end, up from under 5% in 2025 (Writer).
  • Hype vs. reality: deployment is outrunning payoff. 71% of executives at $1B+ revenue companies cite organizational readiness, not technology, as the top barrier to AI performance, and only 29% see significant ROI from generative AI even as individual productivity gains hit 5x (Writer).
  • Microsoft and SAP are reportedly working toward an agent-to-agent bridge between Microsoft 365 Copilot and SAP Joule — a sign the big platforms would rather interoperate than force customers to pick a single AI ecosystem (Collab Collective).

✅ Implementation Wins & Losses

  • Gartner data shows more than 70% of ERP implementations still fail to fully meet their original business goals, and in discrete manufacturing the failure rate hits 73% with average cost overruns of 215% (Godlan).
  • Salesforce Agentforce Life Sciences customers are going live in record time when they use a dedicated org and a certified Agentforce consultant on the project — Salesforce's own playbook for avoiding the usual go-live drag (Salesforce).
  • Post-go-live reality check: S/4HANA transformations typically see a 10-25% productivity dip in the first 30-60 days, and advisors are telling CFOs to budget 15-20% of implementation cost annually for post-go-live support in year one and two (Valantic).

  • "Data gravity" is being called the top cloud migration hurdle of 2026, as the cost of moving accumulated petabytes off a given provider makes switching prohibitively expensive for many enterprises (BuzzClan).
