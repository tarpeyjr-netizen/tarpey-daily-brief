Enterprise Software Intel — Week of November 19, 2026

🏢 Platform News

SAP leadership change: Chief Customer Officer Thomas Saueressig will leave the Executive Board at the end of March 2027, and Emmanuel Raptopoulos takes over his duties on an interim basis from January (SAP News). SAP also closed its TechWolf acquisition. TechWolf's skills "context graph" becomes the core of SuccessFactors and the grounding layer for Joule's HR agents (SAP News).

S/4HANA

AstraZeneca is moving its entire SAP estate from ECC to SAP Cloud ERP Private through RISE with SAP, under its "Axial" programme. SAP calls it one of the largest SAP footprints in life sciences (SAP News). SAP was also named a Leader in Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for Cloud ERP for Service-Centric Enterprises (SAP). Deadline check: ECC mainstream maintenance ends December 31, 2027, about 13 months away. Typical migrations take 18–36 months, so anyone who hasn't started is already looking at extended maintenance (Redress Compliance).

Joule

Following TechEd (Oct 27–29), Joule Work and Joule Studio are reaching general availability in phases:

  • Joule Work adds agentic planning for multistep workflows and A2A/MCP interoperability. A governed agent gateway lets third-party agents call Joule agents.
  • Joule Studio supports 70+ models and adds n8n workflows in an early-adopter program.
  • Custom agents run free on the SAP-managed runtime through December 2026 for select customers (SAP News; ERP Today).

Forrester warns that the design makes it hard for approvers to know what they are actually approving (Forrester).

SAP Analytics Cloud

No SAC news turned up this week, and SAC was largely absent from TechEd coverage, where analytics messaging centered on Business Data Cloud. One example: Bestseller's new people analytics runs on BDC's People Intelligence, not SAC (SAP News). Watch the SAC quarterly release notes and how SAP positions SAC against BDC.

SAP Integration Suite

TechEd brought new AI tools for error analysis, anomaly flagging, schema setup, and Git/GitHub operations. RISE customers will also be able to run Integration Suite in their private cloud through a fully managed Edge Integration Cell, though no dates were given (Computerwoche). An MCP Gateway is on the roadmap (SAP user-group update).

SAP Build

No Build-specific announcement. Joule Studio is now SAP's AI-first development environment, and SAP Learning distinguishes it from "Joule Studio Classic" inside SAP Build. Watch SAP Community and the Build release notes for signs that Build is being folded into Joule Studio.

WalkMe

No news this week. The last update was new authoring and analytics tooling at the September Transformation Excellence Summit (Futurum). Watch for the quarterly release.

Signavio

No news this week. The Process Consulting Agent announced in September is the item to track for availability (Futurum).

LeanIX

No news this week. The expanded AI Enterprise Architect Assistant shown in September is the latest development. ECC-to-RISE application rationalization remains LeanIX's main selling point with deadline-driven customers (Futurum).

BTP

The TechEd roadmap for SAP Business AI on BTP, mostly landing in Q1 2027 (ERP Today):

  • SAP Enterprise AI Gateway: GA planned for Q1 2027
  • An AI Governance Assistant for the EU AI Act
  • SAP-RPT-2.0 anomaly detection
  • Business Data Cloud with Dremio on Iceberg
  • Deeper SAP Databricks integration

SAP and NTT also agreed to cooperate on sovereign cloud with Business AI (SAP News).

IBP

SAP-RPT-TS, a time-series forecasting model for IBP, is now GA. SAP says it was named a Leader in several IDC MarketScapes for supply chain planning. It cites Illumina cutting excess inventory 40% with IBP, a vendor-supplied figure (SAP News).

BRIM

Cloud ALM

No news this week. SAP is positioning Cloud ALM to deploy and monitor agent actions in production, and it added BTP operations coverage earlier this year (Pulse). Watch the Cloud ALM "What's New" page.

Salesforce

At Microsoft Ignite on Nov 17, Salesforce and Microsoft announced "Copilotforce" (Salesforce):

  • Salesforce inside Microsoft Copilot, in public preview
  • Sales Cloud in Teams, in pilot
  • Zero-copy Fabric OneLake federation into Data 360, now GA
  • Accenture as implementation partner and "customer zero"

Informatica's governance and lineage features are now GA inside Copilot, Foundry and Fabric (Salesforce). Agentforce Coworker passed 185,000 users, up 21x in three months, with a $50/user/month option (Salesforce). Salesforce also agreed to acquire voice-AI vendor Cresta (Salesforce).

ServiceNow

On Nov 12, ServiceNow launched the Autonomous CRM Engine, a direct shot at legacy CRM incumbents (read: Salesforce). It maps how a customer's current CRM is used, designs the target state, builds and deploys it, then monitors for drift. ServiceNow claims deployments in "weeks." Datacom and Xactly are early customers, and the engine is in controlled availability with broader release in 2027 (ServiceNow). Brazil release notes add governance policies for ServiceNow Arc usage (ServiceNow Docs).

Workday

A quiet week. Q3 FY27 earnings come after the close on Tuesday, Nov 24 (Workday). The big story is still October's "autopilot" agents for Talent Acquisition, Payroll, Accounting and Deployment, orchestrated by Sana. Talent Acquisition reaches GA in early 2027, and the others go to early adopters in early 2027. Nearly 5,000 customers have activated the Deployment Agent (Workday). Workday is also buying C1.ai for identity governance across people and agents (iTWire).

Microsoft 365

Ignite (Nov 17) made Copilot the front door to Microsoft 365 (Microsoft; RCPmag):

  • Now GA: Copilot Home, which merges Chat and Cowork, and Office in Copilot, with full Word, Excel and PowerPoint plus coauthoring.
  • Rolling out to Frontier customers: Autopilot, an autonomous agent with its own Entra Agent ID and a named human manager.
  • Agent 365 adds cost management and visibility into agents built on Anthropic, Databricks, OpenAI and Salesforce.
  • Auto model routing lets users choose OpenAI or Anthropic models.

⚠️ Threats & Risks

  • SAP November Patch Day: 35 notes, 9 Critical, including four unauthenticated CVSS 10.0 flaws:
    • ICM2SHELL in NetWeaver ICM and Web Dispatcher
    • PROXYGATE in NetWeaver Gateway

    The Gateway and SAML2 flaws have no workaround, so patch now (ERP Today; SecurityOnline).

  • Consolidation continues: SAP closed TechWolf, Salesforce is buying Cresta, Loftware is buying Seagull/BarTender (Securities.io), and Vista is acquiring Acumatica (ChannelE2E).
