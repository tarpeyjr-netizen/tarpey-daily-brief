Enterprise Software Intel — Week of September 24, 2026

🏢 Platform News

S/4HANA

SAP's ECC mainstream maintenance ends December 31, 2027, with extended support only via paid agreements through 2030. Early-2026 data shows legacy ECC use has fallen below 50% of organizations for the first time, but roughly 35% of large enterprises remain stuck in planning. Analysts now call 2026 “the last comfortable year” to start a migration, since a full rollout takes 18–36 months (Computer Weekly, September 2026).

Joule

Six new procurement Joule agents (Category Management, Supplier Management, Requisition, Buying, Receiving, and Services Procurement Assistants) reach general availability this month, part of 14 agents SAP unveiled at SAP Connect. Joule Studio, GA since Q1 2026, is now “Joule Studio 2.0” and anchors the new Business AI Platform's build layer; Joule Work's mobile app is GA, with desktop and agent-to-agent protocol due Q4 2026 (SAP News, May 2026).

SAP Analytics Cloud

The Q2 2026 release added asymmetric reporting, live Azure SQL connectivity, and stronger planning controls, following Q1's native Snowflake connectivity. Joule-powered natural-language queries and automated story generation are now embedded directly in planning workflows (Finext, 2026).

SAP Integration Suite

SAP made its MCP Gateway generally available in July 2026, embedding Model Context Protocol into a new Integration Cell runtime alongside API Management, plus a Developer Hub where APIs, events, and MCPs are registered for AI agents. Advanced Event Mesh also gained lightweight “Micro-Integrations” for real-time on-ramps/off-ramps (SAP Community, 2026).

SAP Build

Joule's AI capabilities are now built into Build Work Zone standard edition for personalized workspace recommendations, while Build Process Automation gained new triggers to bridge SAP and non-SAP systems, and Build Code added Freestyle UI5 app creation via slash commands (SAP Community, 2026).

WalkMe

WalkMe's Q3 2026 release (live August 31) added AI Experiences for building and analytics, expanded Contextual AI Assistance, and brought AI-generated content to Learning Arc. Signavio

SAP Signavio was named a Leader in Gartner's newly created Magic Quadrant for Process Intelligence Platforms (May 2026), its fourth straight year as a Leader. Three 2026 release waves added Process Networks, Process Atoms, object-centric process mining, and direct SAP Datasphere replication for continuous process data feeds (SAP News, May 2026).

LeanIX

SAP LeanIX is being repositioned as the governance backbone of SAP's “Autonomous Enterprise” push, hosting a new AI Agent Hub for discovering and governing AI agents, while Application Rationalization is now framed as the starting point for S/4HANA transformation ahead of the 2027 deadline (SAVIC Technologies, 2026).

BTP

At SAP Sapphire 2026, SAP renamed Business Technology Platform to the SAP Business AI Platform (BAIP), consolidating BTP, Business Data Cloud, and Business Transformation Management into context, build (Joule Studio 2.0), and governance layers. The partner BTP competency was renamed BAIP competency effective June 30, 2026 (ERP Today, 2026).

IBP

SAP Integrated Business Planning release 2608 is live, with AI-assisted demand forecasting, automatic outlier detection, and multi-stage stochastic inventory optimization that vendors claim cut planning cycle times 50–70%. Industry commentary argues IBP customers now need orchestration linking planning to execution, not just optimization (SAPns2, 2026).

BRIM

No major product news this week. Watch for BRIM enhancements tied to usage-based billing and the new Business AI Platform data layer at upcoming SAP events; recent SAP PRESS training (May 2026) has focused on BRIM's subscription/usage-billing modules (SAP PRESS, 2026).

Cloud ALM

Salesforce

ServiceNow's AI business crossed $1 billion in annual contract value in Q2 2026, and the stock climbed roughly 28% in a single month on AI momentum (ad-hoc-news, September 2026).

Workday
