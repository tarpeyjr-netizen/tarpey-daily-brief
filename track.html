Week ending Saturday, August 1, 2026

This Week

July 26–Aug 1: a huge week headlined by the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow and the final day of USATF Outdoor Championships in New York. Josh Kerr won the inaugural men's Commonwealth Mile gold, while Australians Abbey Caldwell and Jessica Hull went 1-2 in the women's mile. In New York, Sophie O'Sullivan and Cian McPhillips both took Irish national titles, with O'Sullivan breaking her mother Sonia's 31-year-old championship record.
