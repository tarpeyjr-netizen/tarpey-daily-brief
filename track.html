Week ending September 5, 2026

This Week

August 30 – September 5, 2026. The Wanda Diamond League wrapped its season with the two-day Memorial Van Damme final in Brussels (Sept 4–5), and eight roster athletes were in it. Standout: Ethan Strand was the top American in a brutal 1500m final, taking third in a season-best 3:30.50 ahead of Jakob Ingebrigtsen, Yared Nuguse and Cole Hocker. Honorable mention to Femke Broeders-Bol, beaten by six hundredths in a 1:54.81 photo finish over 800m. Most of the rest of the roster is on taper — the inaugural World Athletics Ultimate Championships open in Budapest on September 11.
