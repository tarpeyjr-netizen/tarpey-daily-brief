Week ending August 22, 2026

This Week

August 16–22, 2026. The Diamond League restarted after its championship break and Rai Benjamin owned it — 46.67 in the Lausanne 400m hurdles, erasing Ed Moses' 1981 meeting record and beating the field by well over a second, then a 43.83 flat-400 personal best at Silesia a day later. The European Championships closed in Birmingham on the 16th with Andrew Coscoran taking 1500m bronze, and Ethan Strand handed Jakob Ingebrigtsen a loss in the Norwegian's first 1500m back from an 11-month Achilles layoff.

Note: the Silesia Diamond League ran Aug 22–23; its main track program fell on Sunday the 23rd and is included here for completeness.

Week ending Saturday 15, 2026

This Week

August 9–15, 2026. The European Athletics Championships in Birmingham (Aug 10–16) dominated the week and carried nearly all of the roster's action. The standout: Jakob Ingebrigtsen returned from February Achilles surgery to win a fourth straight European 5000m title in 13:15.29 — his first race in 11 months. Andrew Coscoran ran the race of his life for 1500m bronze in 3:36.24. The U.S. contingent was almost entirely dark, sitting between USATF Nationals (late July) and the World Athletics Ultimate Championships in Budapest next month.
