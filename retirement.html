Wilmington

New Hanover County, North Carolina · Cape Fear River port city near the Atlantic beaches
PopulationCity about 127,000 (estimates vary); metro 492,772 (2025) FRED
65+ PopulationMetro about 24.9% vs 18% US (2024); city 17.8% USAFacts
ClimateHumid subtropical; July highs near 88°F, January lows near 38°F, 50–55 in. rain a year
Nearest MountainsAsheville — about 322–359 road miles, roughly 6.5 hours; no nearer range found
Nearest BeachWrightsville Beach — about 10 miles, 15–20 min
Nearest AirportILM — about 3–5 miles from downtown; 26–43 routes depending on source (American, Delta, United, Avelo and others)
Walk ScoreDowntown 82 (Transit 31, Bike 86); citywide not readily available; other neighborhoods 52–67
Crime vs. NationalViolent crime about 8% above US average; violent crime rose in 2024

Cost of Living

Sources disagree sharply on Wilmington’s overall cost of living: HomeSnacks puts it near 109–110 against a US average of 100, while Payscale shows it about 5% below average. Housing is the swing factor, with indexes of 131–134 at HomeSnacks and AreaVibes but 21–23% below average at RentCafe and Payscale. A monthly budget figure is not readily available, so treat the index as unresolved.

Taxes on Retirement Income

North Carolina does not tax Social Security. Pensions and 401(k)/IRA withdrawals are taxed as ordinary income at a flat state rate; third-party sites list 3.99%–4.5% for 2026, so confirm the rate with the NC Department of Revenue. Government pensions vested before August 1989 (the Bailey settlement) are exempt. New Hanover County’s property tax is 30.6 cents per $100 (held after the 2025 reappraisal) Property Tax Almanac, plus the City of Wilmington at 28.25 cents for FY2026 (the FY2027 city rate was not confirmed). Owners 65+ under an income cap can exclude the greater of $25,000 or 50% of appraised value WHQR.

Housing Market

Redfin-based data shows a 2026 median sale price near $451,667, down 2.6% from 2025’s $463,466 Stacker. Zillow’s January 2026 home value index was $406,726, about 1.3% lower year over year OCity, and Trulia shows a typical 3-bedroom near $394,000 Trulia. A specific 2–3 bedroom price-trend percentage and a comparison to the national median were not readily available.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

Hurricanes and flooding dominate: the NWS says a hurricane strikes the Cape Fear area about every six years on average NWS Wilmington, and the area saw a record 102.4 inches of rain in 2018. North Carolina’s 2025–26 settlement raised average homeowners rates 7.5% in June 2025 and another 7.5% in June 2026 WHQR. Standard policies exclude flood, and fewer than 2% of coastal homeowners carry flood coverage.

Sports Proximity

The nearest NHL team, the Carolina Hurricanes in Raleigh, is about 130 miles and 2–2.5 hours away DistanceFrom; the Carolina Panthers in Charlotte are roughly 180–200 miles and 3.5–4 hours. Local college athletics center on UNCW, but a sourced conference detail was not found, so Wilmington is best treated as a beach and cultural hub with big-league sports a day trip away.

Senior Services & Community

Active-adult options include Del Webb Wilmington, The Village at Motts Landing and Porters Neck Plantation Sunlight Living; a named continuing-care community was not confirmed. OLLI at UNCW offers non-credit courses for adults 50+ CommunityFinder, and volunteering runs through RSVP at the New Hanover County Senior Resource Center and the Cape Fear Volunteer Center Caring Senior Service.

Healthcare & Hospitals

The leading local system is Novant Health New Hanover Regional Medical Center, named among North Carolina’s top hospitals in August 2026 WECT. Quality marks are mixed: CMS gave it 3 stars in May 2026, up from 2 WECT, while its Leapfrog safety grade was a C in November 2025 WHQR. Healthgrades reports 56% of patients would recommend it Healthgrades.

Cost of Living

Estimates conflict: blog-level sources put overall costs anywhere from 5% to 15% below the national average, and no official index turned up. Zillow lists average rent near $1,508 as of January 2026, versus a Census (ACS 2023) median rent of $1,155. Treat the index figures as rough and compare line items before relying on them.

Taxes on Retirement Income

Tennessee levies no state tax on Social Security, pensions or retirement-account withdrawals, and the Hall tax on interest and dividends was repealed in 2021 (per Raisin and CountryTaxCalc). Federal tax still applies and sales tax is among the nation's highest. A current Hamilton County property tax rate was not readily available. The county senior property tax freeze has been debated repeatedly, with a vote delayed to December in one undated report, so confirm its status with the county trustee.

Housing Market

Zillow put the typical home value at about $312,219 at the end of January 2026, down 1.2% over the prior year. The Census ACS 2023 median value is lower at $259,200, and listing prices run higher (a median near $399,450 in February 2026), so the gap between asking and sale prices is wide. A 2–3 bedroom-specific trend was not readily available.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

Hamilton County sits on the edge of Dixie Alley, and the 2020 Easter tornado outbreak damaged parts of the city. Flood risk is a separate concern: one aggregator claims far more properties are at flood risk than FEMA maps show, though that is a secondary source. MoneyGeek estimates average homeowners insurance at about $2,958 a year for a 2,500 sq ft home, and standard policies exclude flood. No official FEMA risk index score was found.

Sports Proximity

Verified distances and schedules were not readily available this week. The nearest major-league markets are Atlanta and Nashville, each a drive of roughly two to two and a half hours (general knowledge, unverified). Local options include the Chattanooga Lookouts (minor league baseball) and UT Chattanooga Mocs athletics, also unverified in this week's searches.

Senior Services & Community

Directories list many 55+ options, including Creekside at Shallowford, The Village at Greenway and Alexian Village in Signal Mountain, though the listings are aggregator pages of uneven quality. For volunteering, the Southeast Tennessee Area Agency on Aging and Disability lists senior-center and MyRide TN driver roles. No local continuing-education program for older adults was found.

Healthcare & Hospitals

CHI Memorial was named a U.S. News Best Regional Hospital for the 11th straight year (July 2025 release), ranked fourth in Tennessee and first in Chattanooga. Erlanger is the area's other major system, but no current ranking was found in this week's results.

Cost of Living

Published cost-of-living indexes for Georgetown disagree widely, from about 88 to 117 against a national baseline of 100, and most come from real-estate sites with unclear methods. The high end (117) is driven by housing, while another source puts it at 97.6. Groceries and healthcare are reported as cheaper than average and housing as the main premium. A monthly budget figure is not readily available.

Taxes on Retirement Income

Texas has no state income tax, so Social Security, pensions and 401(k)/IRA withdrawals are not taxed by the state (general knowledge; not confirmed in this week's search results). Property tax is the main burden: the 2024 table lists a county rate of 0.355670 and a city rate of 0.364700 per $100, with a $125,000 county over-65 exemption. The city's FAQ says the over-65 exemption also freezes the school/city tax bill at the qualifying-year amount, but sources disagree on the city exemption amount, so verify with the appraisal district.

Housing Market

Redfin reported a median of about $400K in November 2025, down 5.9% year over year. MLS data cited by Neuhaus Real Estate showed a February 2026 median of $404,990, with new construction heavily influencing the figure. HAR listing snapshots show average list prices drifting from about $575K in May to about $540K in September 2026, and inventory has risen. A 2–3 bedroom-specific trend is not readily available.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

Hail is the dominant risk: a baseball-sized hailstorm damaged thousands of homes in northwest Georgetown near Sun City and Serenada (date not shown in the results). A July 2025 flood led to a city disaster declaration; standard policies exclude flood, so separate coverage matters. Insurers are described as selective in hail-prone Central Texas, and a current average premium is not readily available.

Sports Proximity

Georgetown sits roughly 30 miles north of Austin, so Texas Longhorns athletics and Austin FC (MLS) are the nearest major options; this week's searches did not return verified distances or schedules, so treat that as general knowledge. Southwestern University in town fields NCAA Division III teams. San Antonio (Spurs) and Houston and Dallas pro franchises are a few hours away by car.

Senior Services & Community

Sun City Texas, opened in 1995 as the state's first Sun City, is a Del Webb 55+ community that drives much of the city's growth. AgeFriendly cites about 14,000 residents over 55 there, and listings describe dozens of clubs plus Senior University, which partners with Southwestern University for continuing education. Other options include Grand Living at Georgetown. Volunteer programs were not readily available in the results.

Healthcare & Hospitals

St. David's Georgetown Hospital is part of St. David's Medical Center, which holds the CMS five-star overall rating and was named to Healthgrades' 2026 America's 100 Best Hospitals (system-level, not Georgetown alone). The Georgetown hospital earned a Leapfrog "A" in 2023 per Wilco Sun. Baylor Scott & White Round Rock received a Leapfrog "A" in spring 2025. A U.S. News rating for a Georgetown facility was not found.

Cost of Living

Estimates of Prescott's cost of living vary widely by source, from about 14% above the national average to 25% above (Redfin), and housing is the main driver — one source puts it about 40% above average. A single monthly budget figure is not readily available. Treat these as a range rather than a firm index.

Taxes on Retirement Income

Arizona fully exempts Social Security and exempts military and government retirement income (private pensions get a small per-person exclusion), while other retirement-account withdrawals are generally taxable at the state's flat 2.5% rate (Retirement Living). A Yavapai County effective property tax rate near 0.55% is cited for primary residences, below the ~0.62% statewide average. A Senior Property Valuation Protection option (the “senior freeze”) exists for ages 65+ with limited income; confirm eligibility with the county assessor.

Housing Market

Zillow puts Prescott's typical home value near $607K (down 0.7% year over year through June 2026), and Houzeo shows a ~$600K median sale price that is roughly flat. Trulia lists 3-bedroom homes at an average of about $640K; a specific 2–3 bedroom price-trend percentage for Prescott is not readily available. Neighboring Prescott Valley is cheaper (Zillow ~$462K) but Redfin reported its sale prices down 5–9% in early 2026.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

Wildfire is the dominant risk; risk vendor FireRisk.ai scores Yavapai County 72/100 (“Very High”), though its scores for Prescott vary by area (FireRisk.ai). Local reporting describes homeowners being dropped or non-renewed, and statewide Arizona premiums rose about 48% from 2021 to 2024. Defensible-space work and Firewise certification are the main ways to keep coverage; the Arizona FAIR Plan is the last-resort insurer.

Sports Proximity

Prescott has no major pro franchise; the Phoenix area (Cardinals, Diamondbacks, Suns) is roughly 100 miles south, about a 1.5–2 hour drive (approximate). Locally the signature event is Prescott Frontier Days, billed as the World's Oldest Rodeo (since 1888). College athletics information is not readily available.

Senior Services & Community

Directories list numerous 55+ and age-targeted communities, including Prescott Lakes (~1,300 homes), Yavapai Hills (~1,015), Talking Rock (~611) and Hassayampa Village (55places). Las Fuentes Resort Village offers independent living, assisted living and memory care downtown on month-to-month leases. Continuing-education and volunteer program details are not readily available.

Healthcare & Hospitals

The leading regional system is Yavapai Regional Medical Center (now Dignity Health), with a West campus in Prescott and East campus in Prescott Valley. Fitch upgraded its revenue bonds to “A” (Beckers Hospital Review), but that is a financial rating; a U.S. News, Healthgrades or CMS star rating is not readily available.

Cost of Living

Guides for 2026 put one person at roughly $1,250-$2,500 a month, with about $2,000 for a comfortable retiree life. Rent for a one-bedroom is cited near $1,121 in the historic center versus about $692 outside it, and furnished central apartments run $800-1,500. Groceries average around $380 per person and market shopping is 30-40% cheaper than supermarkets. These are blog and crowdsourced estimates that disagree, so verify locally. See Rio Times and Nomada.

Taxes on Retirement Income

Effective 2026, the Mexican temporary resident visa reportedly requires about $4,400/month in income or roughly $73,000-$74,000 in savings/investments (UMA-indexed; the two routes cannot be combined), applied for at a Mexican consulate; confirm with the consulate or INM. See BorderPilot and ExpatLife. US citizens still file Form 1040; sources say Social Security is taxed only by the US, while 401(k)/IRA distributions may be taxable in Mexico if you become a Mexican tax resident, with the US foreign tax credit offsetting double tax, and there is no totalization agreement. See Greenback Tax Services. The predial (property tax) is modest, a few hundred dollars a year on median homes, with a 15% discount for paying by January per a local broker (see San Miguel Real Estate). Consult a cross-border CPA.

Housing Market

The local MLS tracker reports a $384K median sale price for all closings in January-June 2026, with a 96% sale-to-ask ratio (Live in San Miguel). One data vendor claims prices rose about 8-10% over the 12 months to June 2026 (7-10% for condos/townhouses), but its methodology is not disclosed (Latinvestor). A separate broker report said resale unit sales rose 8% year over year in January 2026 (Hardcastle). A 2-3 bedroom-specific trend is not readily available. Price per square meter estimates conflict widely.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

ThinkHazard rates earthquake hazard for Guanajuato as medium (10% chance of damaging shaking in 50 years) and cyclone hazard as high on the Spanish page; as an inland town the main exposure is heavy rain and flooding rather than coastal storm surge. See ThinkHazard. Homeowner insurance cost data and local flood-zone information: not readily available.

Sports Proximity

The nearest Liga MX clubs are Querétaro FC (Gallos Blancos) at Estadio Corregidora, in Querétaro city about 1.5 hours away, and Club León in León, with a regional rivalry called the Clásico del Bajío; see Wikipedia. The Mexico City Grand Prix is scheduled for October 30-November 1, 2026 at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez (RacingNews365); drive time from San Miguel is not readily available in the sources found. Other major-league US-style sports: not readily available.

Senior Services & Community

San Miguel is a well-known expat and retiree hub, with an English-speaking community and many expat-oriented services, though the expat population count is not readily available. Guides describe public IMSS care plus private options, and the temporary resident visa is the main route for retirees (renewable to four years, then permanent residency). See Live in San Miguel and ExpatExchange. Dedicated senior-service program details: not readily available.

Healthcare & Hospitals

Private options named in expat guides include Hospital MAC (opened 2017), Hospital Joya and Hospital San Miguel Arcángel, while public IMSS facilities are described as crowded. Expats often go to Querétaro (about 1.5 hours) for specialized care or surgery. See ExpatExchange and Hospital MAC. No JCI accreditation for any local hospital was found, and a Numbeo Healthcare Index for the city is not readily available.

Population~90,551 city · ~157,800 metro (Santa Fe County)

Cost of Living

Santa Fe is pricier than the national baseline: cost-of-living trackers such as Oysterlink and HomeSnacks put the overall index roughly 12–18% above the US average, driven by housing, whose index runs about 142–161. Utilities, healthcare and transportation are reported below average. Rent averages about $1,650 for an 836 sq ft apartment, and a single all-in monthly budget figure was not readily available.

Taxes on Retirement Income

New Mexico exempts Social Security for filers with federal AGI under $100,000 (single) or $150,000 (joint), and taxes it to the extent it is taxable federally above those limits, according to Brevy. Pension, IRA and 401(k) withdrawals are taxed at graduated rates of 1.5% to 5.9%, though filers 65+ can claim an exemption of up to $8,000 that phases out as income rises. The statewide effective property tax rate is about 0.67% on one-third of assessed value, and senior relief includes a valuation freeze for 65+ with income under about $44,700 (2025) and a $2,000 head-of-family exemption. These are secondary sources; verify with the NM Taxation and Revenue Department.

Housing Market

Zillow puts the typical Santa Fe-area home value at $581,279 in August 2026, up 0.8% year over year, with a median sale price of $675,583. Redfin shows Santa Fe County's median sale price at $631K in June, down 4.4% from a year earlier, while Downtown runs about $1.1M, up 7.8%. A comparison against the national median and a specific 2–3 bedroom trend were not readily available.

Natural Disaster Risk & Insurance

Wildfire is the dominant risk: the 2022 Hermits Peak/Calf Canyon fire burned more than 340,000 acres, the largest in state history. According to Fire Adapted New Mexico and Insurify, insurers are raising rates or dropping coverage in wildfire-prone areas, including the Greater Santa Fe Fireshed, so check a specific address’s wildfire designation before buying. A FEMA National Risk Index score for Santa Fe was not readily available.

Sports Proximity

Santa Fe has no major-league franchise; the nearest pro teams are in Albuquerque, about 60 miles and an hour away, per SantaFe.com: the Albuquerque Isotopes (Triple-A, Colorado Rockies affiliate) and New Mexico United of the USL Championship, both at Isotopes Park. The University of New Mexico Lobos (Division I, Mountain West) play football at University Stadium and basketball at The Pit, also an hour down I-25. Locally, the independent Pecos League’s Santa Fe Fuego play at Fort Marcy Ballfield.

Senior Services & Community

El Castillo is a downtown Life Plan Community offering independent living through nursing care, and Las Campanas is a gated, retiree-popular master-planned community with two Nicklaus golf courses (not a CCRC). Senior living starts from about $5,310 a month in 2026. For continuing education, UNM Extended University has a Santa Fe presence and Santa Fe Community College is local; no Santa Fe OLLI program turned up, and a volunteer-opportunity roster was not readily available.

Healthcare & Hospitals

CHRISTUS St. Vincent Regional Medical Center is the area’s leading hospital and carries a 5-star CMS overall rating. U.S. News rates it “high performing” in five adult procedures (heart failure, hip fracture, pacemaker, pneumonia and stroke), with patient experience at 3 of 5.
