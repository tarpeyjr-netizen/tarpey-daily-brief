Wikipedia Article of the Day

Europejara — Europejara is a genus of tapejarid pterosaur, a flying reptile that lived during the Barremian age of the Early Cretaceous epoch in what is now Spain.

Interesting fact: Its lower-jaw crest was about four times deeper than the back of the lower jaw, and the wingspan estimate was revised from about 2 m in 2012 to 3–4 m in 2023.
