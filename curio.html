Wikipedia Article of the Day

Battle of Schliengen — The Battle of Schliengen took place on 24 October 1796 between the French Army of the Rhine and Moselle and the Austrian army near the village of Schliengen during the War of the First Coalition.

Interesting fact: Even after losing, Moreau proposed a truce and Archduke Charles was inclined to accept, but Emperor Francis II and the Aulic Council refused any armistice. That forced Charles into sieges at Kehl and Hüningen that tied up his army on the Rhine for most of the winter, while Moreau still had enough spare troops to send 14 demi-brigades (about 12,000 men) to Italy to help at Mantua.
