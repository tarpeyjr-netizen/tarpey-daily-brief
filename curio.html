Wikipedia Article of the Day

Hongwu Emperor — The Hongwu Emperor, personal name Zhu Yuanzhang, was the founding emperor of the Ming dynasty of China, reigning from 1368 to 1398.

Interesting fact: After the chancellor Hu Weiyong was executed for treason in 1380, the emperor abolished the office of grand chancellor altogether and forbade it ever being restored, placing the six ministries directly under his own control; one of his imperial guard units, the Embroidered Uniform Guard, doubled as a secret police.
