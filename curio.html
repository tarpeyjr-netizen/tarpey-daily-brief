Wikipedia Article of the Day

W. S. Gilbert — Sir William Schwenck Gilbert was an English dramatist, librettist, poet and illustrator best known for his collaboration with the composer Arthur Sullivan, which produced fourteen comic operas.

Interesting fact: Gilbert had a telephone installed in 1882 linking his home to the prompt desk at the Savoy Theatre so he could monitor performances from his study — and he had already mentioned the device in H.M.S. Pinafore in 1878, before London even had telephone service.

Wikipedia Article of the Day

Aucanquilcha — Aucanquilcha is a large compound volcano in the Antofagasta Region of northern Chile, within the Central Volcanic Zone of the Andes.

Interesting fact: A sulfur mine near its summit, at about 5,950 m, was once the highest mine in the world; its ore was first carried down by llamas, later by trucks and an aerial cableway, and in 1986 caretakers living in a hut just below it were the highest permanent human inhabitants on Earth.
