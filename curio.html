Wikipedia Article of the Day

Mairzy Doates — Mairzy Doates was an American Thoroughbred racehorse and multiple graded stakes winner, most remembered for her victory at the inaugural Japan Cup in 1981.

Interesting fact: On the morning of the 1981 Japan Cup, her owner and his associate demanded the track be watered because the ground was too firm; officials appeared to agree, but the sprinklers they pointed to only watered the flowerbeds. She won anyway, by one length in 2:25.3, a full second under the previous course record.
