Miami Review

Review: Miami 33, Wake Forest 20

No. 5 Miami improved to 3-0 (2-0 ACC) behind a record-setting night from Darian Mensah, who completed his first 24 passes — the second-most consecutive completions in a single FBS game on record — and finished 30-of-34 for 220 yards and three touchdowns, including a 9-yard score to Joshua Moore and a 21-yard strike to Elija Lofton. The streak extended to 35 straight completions dating back to last week's win over Florida A&M, breaking the ACC multi-game record of 30 set by North Carolina's Mitch Trubisky in 2016. Miami raced to a 31-7 lead midway through the third quarter before the offense stalled with four straight three-and-outs, but the defense answered with four consecutive fourth-quarter stops to close out Wake Forest (2-1, 0-1 ACC), which was flagged for 13 penalties. Miami is off next Saturday's crowded slate and returns home to face Central Michigan on Sept. 26.

Texas Tech Review

Review: Texas Tech 28, Houston 26

The Red Raiders trailed 10-0 just nine-and-a-half minutes in, but running back J'Koby Williams broke off runs of 19, 5 and 29 yards early in the second half — the last untouched through the left side to make it 21-17 — and Cameron Dickey added a 1-yard touchdown with 7:36 left. Texas Tech is 3-0 (1-0 Big 12); Houston falls to 2-1 (0-1). The Red Raiders host Sam Houston on Sept. 26.

Review: Texas A&M 50, Missouri State 0

The Aggies opened 2026 with a wire-to-wire shutout at Kyle Field, scoring on the opening drive and never letting the Bears cross midfield with any consequence. Marcel Reed was efficient rather than spectacular — 21-of-30 for 233 yards and two touchdowns, plus 20 rushing yards — while ten different Aggies caught a pass. Mario Craver (5 catches, 41 yards, TD) and Isaiah Horton (4 catches, 76 yards, TD) led the receivers; Rueben Owens II ran 17 times for 77 yards behind a 236-yard team rushing effort.

The turning point was less a moment than an accumulation: a second-quarter muffed punt that Missouri State illegally kicked through the back of the end zone for a safety turned a 7-0 game into a rout. Texas A&M is 1-0 and should hold at or near No. 8 with a full-strength Arizona State test at noon next Saturday.

Penn State Nittany Lions No. 18 · Big Ten Review

Review: Penn State 45, Marshall 0

Matt Campbell’s first game as Penn State’s head coach could hardly have gone better: a 45-0 Stripe-Out shutout in front of 108,489 at Beaver Stadium, and the first shutout in a Penn State head coach’s debut since Dick Harlow blanked Westminster 26-0 in 1915. Rocco Becht completed 13 of 18 for 271 yards and a career-high four touchdown passes; six different Nittany Lions scored touchdowns.

Koby Howard was the headliner with 161 receiving yards and the game’s first touchdown, a 42-yard strike four plays into the offense’s opening possession. Tony Rojas (seven tackles) and Caleb Bacon (six tackles, three for loss) each had a sack as the defense capped Marshall at 147 yards, and freshman safety Bryson Williams’ interception set up the final score. Penn State is 1-0 heading to Philadelphia.

  • Campbell became the first Penn State head coach in 111 years to open his tenure with a shutout. (GoPSUsports.com, Sept. 5)
  • Becht’s four touchdown passes were a career high, and backup Alex Manske handled the entire fourth quarter, going 6-for-6 in relief for 57 yards. (GoPSUsports.com, Sept. 5)
  • The running back rotation stayed deliberately wide: Carson Hansen (48 yards), Cam Wallace (42 yards, TD), Quinton Martin Jr. (38 yards, TD) and James Peoples (31 yards) all took double-digit or near-double-digit shares. (GoPSUsports.com, Sept. 5)

  • The program opened the Paterno Family Football Experience Room at Beaver Stadium during opening week. (GoPSUsports.com, Sept. 1)
