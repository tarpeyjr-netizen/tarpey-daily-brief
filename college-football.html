Next: Sat, Oct 17 vs. The Citadel · 1:00 p.m. ET · SEC Network+ · Kyle Field, College Station, Texas

  • Jamal Roberts ran for a career-high 222 yards and two touchdowns for the Tigers, while A&M managed 29 rushing yards on 23 carries (ESPN, Oct. 10).

  • The school posted its game recap, “Football Drops 27-6 Decision to No. 14 Missouri” (12thMan.com, Oct. 10).

Review: Missouri 27, Texas A&M 6

Missouri controlled the line of scrimmage, out-gaining A&M 399 yards to 216 and rushing for 252 yards on 5.7 per carry against 1.3 per carry for the Aggies. A&M went 5-of-18 on third down, committed seven penalties for 50 yards and turned the ball over twice. Roberts (34 carries, 222 yards, two touchdowns) was the difference, and Cayden Lee caught seven passes for 94 yards and a score; for A&M, TK Norman had three catches for 55 yards and KJ Edwards, a two-touchdown rusher against Arkansas, had 20 yards on six carries. The Aggies are 3-3 with a home game against The Citadel next before the trip to Alabama.

Penn State Big Ten Review

Next: Sat, Oct 17 at Michigan · 3:30 p.m. ET · TV not listed in ESPN schedule · Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor, Mich.

Review: USC 31, Penn State 23

Penn State tied the game at 7, 14 and 17 and led 20-17 and 23-17 on Ryan Barker field goals, but USC answered with a 13-yard King Miller touchdown run and a 10-yard Maiava pass to Kayden Dixon-Wyatt to pull away. Trent Mosley caught nine passes for 111 yards and a score and Miller rushed for 102 yards for USC. The Lions ran for only 68 yards (2.2 per carry) and went 2-of-12 on third down, though they converted all five fourth-down tries. Penn State is 3-3 heading to Michigan on Oct. 17.
