Miami Pre-Game

Pre-Game: at Stanford

No. 7 Miami (0-0) opens on the West Coast Friday at 9 p.m. ET on ESPN at Stanford Stadium against a Stanford team also 0-0. The consensus line is Miami −24.5 with a total of 48.5, the largest spread on any tracked team's Week 1 board and a reflection of how far apart the two programs are projected. Miami is the highest-ranked ACC team in the AP preseason poll and the only ACC program in the top 10, so the margin matters as much as the result for a team that will be judged on playoff résumé from the first snap. A stumble in a late-night road opener against an unranked opponent would be the kind of loss that follows a team into December.

Georgia Tech Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Colorado

Georgia Tech (0-0) hosts Colorado (0-0) tonight at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN at Bobby Dodd Stadium, with the consensus line Georgia Tech −6.5 and a total of 50.5. It is the back half of a home-and-home the Yellow Jackets won 27–20 in Boulder last season, and it brings Deion Sanders to Atlanta. Colorado is coming off 3-9 and has rebuilt nearly its entire two-deep on defense, with Julian Lewis at quarterback; Tech breaks in a new offensive coordinator and a new starter under center, with Michigan transfer running back Justice Haynes the headline addition. Tech received four votes in the AP preseason poll — a win over a Big 12 opponent on national television is the quickest path onto the ballot.

Minnesota Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Eastern Illinois

Minnesota opens P.J. Fleck's 10th season tonight at 8 p.m. ET against Eastern Illinois at Huntington Bank Stadium, streaming on Peacock. The Gophers are a 42.5-point favorite with a total of 51.5 and have beaten their last two FCS opponents by a combined 114–0. Quarterback Drake Lindsey has not thrown an interception in five straight games, tied for the longest active streak in the FBS. Eastern Illinois arrives 1-0 after a 28–14 win over Murray State. Minnesota picked up five votes in the AP preseason poll; a clean, quick win is the only outcome that keeps that momentum.

College of Wooster Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Wilmington

Wooster opens at home tonight at 7 p.m. ET against Wilmington in year two under head coach and 2010 alumnus Austin Holter. It is the Scots' fourth straight opener against the Quakers, who won the most recent meeting 59–35. Wooster returns a veteran core and added more than 40 freshmen for depth, but the receiving room is the question after graduating its two leading playmakers; junior Chance Bennett is the only returnee with more than 25 catches. North Coast Athletic Conference play begins Sep. 12 at Kenyon.

Georgia Tech Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Colorado

Georgia Tech (0-0) hosts Colorado (0-0) Thursday at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN at Bobby Dodd Stadium, closing a home-and-home the Yellow Jackets won 27–20 in Boulder last season; the consensus line is Georgia Tech -6.5, total 50.5. The subplot is Deion Sanders' return to Atlanta — Brent Key called him “the biggest sports celebrity probably to ever play in the town.” Colorado arrives almost entirely rebuilt after a 3-9 season, with roughly 22 of 24 defensive two-deep spots and three offensive line starts going to newcomers. Tech breaks in new offensive coordinator George Godsey and a new starting quarterback, with Michigan transfer running back Justice Haynes the headline addition; Colorado counters with Carrollton, Ga., native Julian Lewis at quarterback, who did not play in last year's meeting. A win puts Tech at 1-0 with a marquee scalp before ACC play; a loss to a rebuilding Big 12 team would sour a season with real division expectations.
