Penn State Big Ten Pre-Game

Next: Sat, Oct 31 vs. Purdue · 4:15 p.m. ET · BTN · Beaver Stadium, University Park, Pa.

Pre-Game: Purdue

  • Setup: Penn State (3-4, 16th in the Big Ten standings) hosts Purdue (3-4). Neither team is ranked. Penn State is a 10-point favorite (ESPN odds feed, Oct. 30). Penn State leads the series 17-3-1 and has won 11 straight since 2005 (GoPSUSports.com game notes, Oct. 26).
  • Storyline 1, Tony Rojas: The linebacker was named a Butkus Award semifinalist Thursday and added to the Bednarik watch list Wednesday. He leads the team with 56 tackles, 10.5 tackles for loss, five sacks and two interceptions (GoPSUSports.com, Oct. 28-29).
  • Storyline 2, bounce back: Penn State lost 21-18 at Michigan on Oct. 17, then had a bye. Coach Matt Campbell named the pass rush and the running game as bye-week focus areas (GoPSUSports.com, Oct. 26-27).
  • Storyline 3, Chase Sowell: The receiver had 11 catches for 151 yards at Michigan and is the main target for quarterback Rocco Becht (GoPSUSports.com, Oct. 26).
  • Injuries: Guard Brett Eskildsen is "100% ready to go"; Koby Howard was questionable but practiced Tuesday (GoPSUSports.com, Oct. 27).
  • Stakes: A win moves Penn State to 4-4 with Washington (Nov. 7) next. A loss would drop Penn State to 3-5 with a trip to Washington next.

Villanova Patriot League Pre-Game

Next: Sat, Oct 31 at Lafayette · 12:30 p.m. ET · ESPN+ · Fisher Stadium, Easton, Pa.

Pre-Game: Lafayette

  • Setup: Villanova (4-4, fifth in the Patriot League) visits Lafayette (3-4, seventh). Lafayette has won two straight, 31-17 at Fordham on Oct. 24 and 20-7 at Bucknell on Oct. 17 (ESPN schedule and results).
  • Momentum: Villanova beat Lehigh 24-18 on Oct. 24 when Garrett Rogan sacked Derek Morgan on third-and-goal from the 2 on the final play, the Wildcats' second straight win over Lehigh. They also won 38-17 at Fordham on Oct. 17 behind quarterback Pat McQuaide (AP via ESPN, Oct. 24).
  • Storyline: Lafayette's offense has put up 30-plus in three games, including 31 in a 40-31 loss to Richmond and 30 in a 38-30 loss to Holy Cross, so Villanova's defense will be tested (ESPN schedule).
  • Injuries and line: No injury report or betting line was found for this FCS game.
  • Stakes: A win puts Villanova at 5-4 with Richmond at home Nov. 7. A loss would leave the Wildcats at 4-5 with Holy Cross and Georgetown left.
