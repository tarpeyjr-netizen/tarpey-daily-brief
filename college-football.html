Georgia Tech Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Colorado

Georgia Tech (0-0) hosts Colorado (0-0) Thursday at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN at Bobby Dodd Stadium, closing a home-and-home the Yellow Jackets won 27–20 in Boulder last season; the consensus line is Georgia Tech -6.5, total 50.5. The subplot is Deion Sanders' return to Atlanta — Brent Key called him “the biggest sports celebrity probably to ever play in the town.” Colorado arrives almost entirely rebuilt after a 3-9 season, with roughly 22 of 24 defensive two-deep spots and three offensive line starts going to newcomers. Tech breaks in new offensive coordinator George Godsey and a new starting quarterback, with Michigan transfer running back Justice Haynes the headline addition; Colorado counters with Carrollton, Ga., native Julian Lewis at quarterback, who did not play in last year's meeting. A win puts Tech at 1-0 with a marquee scalp before ACC play; a loss to a rebuilding Big 12 team would sour a season with real division expectations.

Minnesota Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Eastern Illinois

Minnesota opens its 143rd season and P.J. Fleck's 10th on Thursday at 8 p.m. ET against Eastern Illinois at Huntington Bank Stadium, streaming on Peacock. The Gophers are a 42.5-point favorite with a total of 51.5, and they have won their last two games against FCS opponents by a combined 114–0. Quarterback Drake Lindsey is the reason for the optimism: he has thrown zero interceptions in five straight games, tied for the longest active streak in the FBS. Eastern Illinois is 1-0 after a 28–14 win over Murray State but is coming off a 3-9 campaign. Anything but a comfortable win would be a genuine alarm for a team picked to contend in the Big Ten's middle tier.

College of Wooster Pre-Game

Pre-Game: Wilmington

Wooster opens at home Thursday at 7 p.m. against Wilmington in year two under 2010 alumnus Austin Holter — the Scots' fourth straight opener against the Quakers, and Wilmington won the last one 59–35. Wooster returns a veteran core and added more than 40 freshmen for depth, but the receiving room is the question mark after graduating its two biggest playmakers; junior Chance Bennett is the lone returnee with more than 25 catches last fall. NCAC play begins Sep. 12 at Kenyon.
