Texas Tech Review

No. 11 Texas Tech routed Sam Houston 49-14 at home to move to 4-0, its best start since 2011-13. Linebacker Austin Romaine returned two interceptions for touchdowns on consecutive Sam Houston snaps, the first FBS player to do that in 13 years, and running back Cameron Dickey scored twice as the Red Raiders built a 21-0 lead just 13 seconds into the second quarter. Quarterback Will Hammond threw three interceptions in the final 5:30 of the first half but still finished 17-of-29 for 224 yards with a touchdown as Tech pulled away in the second half. Texas Tech now heads to Colorado on Oct. 3.

Duke Review
