Review: Kentucky 31, Texas A&M 21

No. 9 Texas A&M's SEC opener unraveled after halftime. The Aggies were tied 7-7 at the break, but Kentucky scored 21 unanswered points in the third quarter — a 42-yard Kenny Minchey-to-Kenny Darby touchdown followed by two short touchdown runs — to go up 28-7 and never trailed again. Reed cut it to 28-14 on a 5-yard touchdown run with about 11:30 left, but a second interception at the Kentucky 7-yard line ended the closest the Aggies would get. Texas A&M outgained Kentucky for stretches but couldn't overcome the third-quarter collapse and a defense that allowed more than 400 total yards. The loss drops A&M to 2-1 heading into a road trip to Baton Rouge to face LSU on Sept. 26.
