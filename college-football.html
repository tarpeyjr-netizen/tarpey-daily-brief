Texas A&M Aggies SEC Review

Next: Sat., Oct. 10 at Missouri · 12:00 PM ET (11:00 AM CT) · ABC · Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium, Columbia, MO

Review: Texas A&M 34, Arkansas 7

  • How it went: A&M broke a two-game skid with its first SEC win of the year, 34-7 before a season-best 108,657 at Kyle Field. Safety Dalton Brooks intercepted a pass on the second snap, and Carsyn Baker scored from 2 yards on the opening drive. The Aggies led 24-7 at halftime and outgained Arkansas 401-214. (12thMan.com, Oct. 3)
