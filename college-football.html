Texas A&M Aggies SEC Review

Next: Sat., Oct. 10 at Missouri · 12:00 PM ET (11:00 AM CT) · ABC · Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium, Columbia, MO

Review: Texas A&M 34, Arkansas 7

  • How it went: A&M broke a two-game skid with its first SEC win of the year, 34-7 before a season-best 108,657 at Kyle Field. Safety Dalton Brooks intercepted a pass on the second snap, and Carsyn Baker scored from 2 yards on the opening drive. The Aggies led 24-7 at halftime and outgained Arkansas 401-214. (12thMan.com, Oct. 3)
  • Top performers: QB Marcel Reed was 13-of-19 for 170 yards and ran for 66 yards and a score. Freshman KJ Edwards ran for two touchdowns (including a 20-yarder) and Baker for one; the Aggies ran for 231 yards and four TDs on 49 carries. K David Olano hit field goals of 57 (a career long, as the first half expired) and 50 yards. Safety Tawfiq Byard and LB Daymion Sanford led the defense with six tackles each. (12thMan.com, ESPN/AP, Oct. 3-4)
  • Turning point: Brooks' interception on Arkansas' first snap set up an immediate touchdown, and the Razorbacks managed only 103 rushing yards and went 3-of-10 on third down. (12thMan.com, Oct. 3)
  • What it means: A&M improves to 3-2 overall and 1-2 in the SEC. The win was the program's 800th, and the seven points allowed were the fewest against Arkansas since Nov. 16, 1991. (12thMan.com, Oct. 3)
  • What's next: A&M visits Missouri on Oct. 10. The Tigers (No. 25 in the Week 5 poll) beat No. 8 Florida 45-17 on Saturday. A&M then hosts The Citadel on Oct. 17 and plays at Alabama on Oct. 24. (12thMan.com, ESPN)
  • Coach Mike Elko: "It was good to bounce back. It was good to get back in the win column and push this thing back in a positive direction." (12thMan.com, Oct. 3)
  • Edwards and Baker, nicknamed "Sonic and Knuckles" by strength coach Tommy Moffitt, are the first A&M freshman duo to score rushing touchdowns in the same game since Sept. 16, 2017. (KBTX, 12thMan.com, Oct. 3-4)

Texas Tech Review

No. 12 Texas Tech beat Colorado 29-7 in Boulder to move to 5-0, helped by four interceptions of Isaiah Wilson, including a pick-six by linebacker John Curry in the end zone. Colorado led 7-3 after the first quarter before Tech scored 26 unanswered, 17 of them in the fourth. QB Will Hammond was 17-of-26 for 134 yards and a touchdown, J'Koby Williams ran for 88 yards and Terrance Carter Jr. had eight catches for 67 yards and a TD. Tech has no game listed for Oct. 10 and next plays Arizona State on Oct. 17.

Miami Review

No. 4 Miami routed Clemson 41-13 at Memorial Stadium to move to 5-0 and take sole possession of first place in the ACC. QB Darian Mensah was 18-of-27 for 227 yards and a touchdown and added a 34-yard scoring run, Mark Fletcher Jr. ran for 77 yards and two TDs, and Cooper Barkate had five catches for 98 yards and a score. Damon Wilson II had 2.5 sacks. The Hurricanes have no game listed for Oct. 10 and host Florida State on Oct. 17.

Florida Review

No. 8 Florida fell 45-17 at No. 25 Missouri, dropping to 4-1 after entering as a 5.5-point favorite. Missouri's Jamal Roberts ran for 211 yards and three TDs and Austin Simmons threw for 340 yards and two scores, snapping the Tigers' nine-game losing streak against ranked opponents. Aaron Philo was 21-of-34 for 197 yards and a TD for Florida, which trailed 20-10 at halftime. The Gators host South Carolina on Oct. 10 and visit Texas on Oct. 17.

Minnesota Review

Minnesota beat Michigan 20-14 at Huntington Bank Stadium to improve to 4-1, despite entering as a 6.5-point underdog. Drake Lindsey was 17-of-25 for 136 yards with a TD and two interceptions, TJ Thomas ran for 56 yards, and TJ Bush Jr. had two sacks. The Gophers' defense intercepted Michigan's Bryce Underwood twice, and the Wolverines (3-2) scored both of their touchdowns in the second quarter. Minnesota goes to Purdue on Oct. 10.

North Carolina Review

No. 3 Notre Dame beat North Carolina 37-26 in rain at Kenan Stadium for its 15th straight win, as CJ Carr threw for 234 yards and four TDs and Jordan Faison had eight catches for 132 yards and two scores. UNC's Billy Edwards Jr. was 17-of-28 for 208 yards and three TDs with two interceptions, and Jordan Shipp had seven catches for 109 yards. The Tar Heels (2-2) go to Pittsburgh on Oct. 10.

College of Wooster Review

Wooster (1-4) was shut out 14-0 at Ohio Wesleyan on Saturday. The Battling Bishops opened with an onside-kick recovery and a 4-yard Brady Morrell-to-Will Kepple touchdown pass, then held the Scots scoreless behind an interception by Connor Stephens and a fumble recovery by Ya'Va Harris. Wooster hosts Oberlin on Oct. 17 and plays at John Carroll on Oct. 24.

Duke and Georgia Tech were off Oct. 3 and meet Oct. 10 at Bobby Dodd Stadium. No secondary team plays Monday, so no previews run today.
