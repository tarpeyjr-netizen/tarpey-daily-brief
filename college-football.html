Pre-Game: LSU

No. 23 Texas A&M (2-1) visits No. 10 LSU (2-1) Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET (6:30 p.m. CT) on ABC at Tiger Stadium, with LSU favored by 9.5 and the total at 52.5. It's the first meeting between Mike Elko and first-year LSU coach Lane Kiffin, who has publicly flagged his defense's past struggles containing Marcel Reed. Key storylines: whether A&M's offensive line can protect Reed in a hostile road environment; the health of Bethel-Roman and Morrow, both questionable for a second straight week; LSU's own banged-up skill corps after Trey'Dez Green's and Dashawn Spears's knee injuries against Ole Miss (Spears out for the year); and whether either quarterback — Reed or LSU's Sam Leavitt — can find consistency after uneven performances. A loss would be Texas A&M's second in three games and drop the Aggies further from playoff position; a win would be a marquee response to the Kentucky upset and keep A&M's SEC hopes alive.

Pre-Game: Wisconsin

Texas Tech Preview

No. 11 Texas Tech (3-0, 1-0 Big 12) hosts Sam Houston (2-1) Saturday at 11 a.m. ET, nationally on TNT/TruTV/HBO Max from Lubbock. The Red Raiders are big favorites off last week's 28-26 escape against Houston, in which running back J'Koby Williams and quarterback Will Hammond carried the offense. Sam Houston enters off its first win of the season, over Nicholls State, but is a heavy step down in competition. The main storyline is whether Texas Tech treats this as a get-well trap game before Big 12 play resumes, or comes out with the same physicality that has it 3-0.

Duke Preview

Duke (3-0, 1-0 ACC) hosts William & Mary (2-2, 1-2 Patriot League) Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on ACC Network Extra at Wallace Wade Stadium, favored by roughly 43.5 points. The Blue Devils are coming off a 35-7 win over Stanford to open ACC play and are chasing a 4-0 start and an eight-game winning streak dating to last season. William & Mary enters on a two-game skid, its most recent a 52-27 loss to Lehigh. Duke coach Manny Diaz has cautioned that W&M's defense compares more to Stanford's than some earlier non-conference opponents, but the Blue Devils are expected to be too much across the board.

Florida Preview

No. 21 Florida (3-0, 1-0 SEC) hosts No. 4 Ole Miss (3-0, 1-0 SEC) Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, with the Gators favored by 3.5 and the total at 60. It's Florida's first Top-5 home test of Jon Sumrall's tenure after a 44-39 shootout win at Auburn behind running back Jadan Baugh, the SEC's leading rusher (458 yards, eight touchdowns). Ole Miss arrives fresh off a 32-24 win over then-No. 7 LSU, with quarterback Trinidad Chambliss leading the SEC in passing. A win would be a signature moment for Florida's rebuild; a loss would be Florida's first of the season but wouldn't be considered a major setback against a top-five team.

Miami Preview

No. 6 Miami (3-0, 2-0 ACC) hosts Central Michigan (2-1) Saturday at 6:30 p.m. ET on The CW at Hard Rock Stadium, and is roughly a 42-point favorite with a 99% win expectancy per ESPN. Quarterback Darian Mensah enters off a record-setting night against Wake Forest in which he completed his first 24 passes, an ACC record for consecutive completions across games. Central Michigan is 117th nationally per SP+ and figures to be overmatched, making the main intrigue whether Miami rests starters early and how much the Hurricanes' offense pads its national efficiency numbers.

Georgia Tech Preview

Georgia Tech (1-2) travels to Stanford (1-2) Saturday at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, with the Yellow Jackets favored by about 4.5. Georgia Tech snapped an 0-2 start with a 44-11 win over FCS Mercer behind quarterback Mendoza (249 yards, three touchdowns) and running back Justice Haynes, but enters just 86th nationally in turnover margin. Stanford, also 1-2, ranks 125th in third-down defense, which could open things up for Georgia Tech's passing game. Both teams are looking to build a winning streak before conference play intensifies.

Minnesota Preview

Minnesota (2-1) opens Big Ten play at Washington (3-0) Saturday, with kickoff at 8 p.m. PT (11 p.m. ET) on FOX. Washington is undefeated and a heavy favorite (roughly 9.5-10.5 points, total 45.5), while Minnesota enters off a 41-7 win over Akron in which quarterback Drake Lindsey threw for 204 yards and three touchdowns. The Gophers will need a much-improved performance on the road against an unbeaten Big Ten opponent to avoid an 0-1 start in conference play.
