Pre-Game: No. 5 Texas

The setup: Texas (9-2, 6-2 SEC) is No. 5 in the AP poll and No. 3 in the CFP rankings. Texas A&M (4-7, 1-7) has lost four in a row. Kickoff is 7:30 PM ET (6:30 local) on ABC at Kyle Field. ESPN's forecast is 61 degrees with gusts to 17 mph. DraftKings, via ESPN, has Texas favored by 10.5 with a total of 40.5. The moneylines are Texas -455 and A&M +350. This is the 121st meeting. Texas leads the series 78-37-5 and has won three straight. A&M has not beaten Texas at home since a 38-30 win on Nov. 23, 2007. (AP/ESPN; 12thMan.com)

  • A&M's quarterback vs. Colin Simmons. Simmons, Texas's edge rusher, has played in only seven games this season. He still has 10.5 sacks, including 5.5 against Tennessee. He faces an A&M line that is missing Ivy and Baugh. Reed (1,712 yards, 8 TD, 9 INT) was benched for Hart, who went 8 of 20 for 131 yards with one touchdown and one interception at Oklahoma. (SI; ESPN team stats)
  • A&M's pass defense vs. Arch Manning. The pass defense has been one of the Aggies' few strengths. It ranks 30th nationally at 190.4 passing yards allowed per game. Manning has 2,485 yards, 21 touchdowns and 10 interceptions. Ryan Wingo (54 catches, 659 yards) leads the Texas receivers, and Raleek Brown (585 yards) leads the run game. (AP/ESPN)
  • A thin receiver group. With Norman, Bussey and Bethel-Roman out, Mario Craver (43 catches, 438 yards, 3 TD) carries the passing game. Kelshaun Johnson's status is the one to watch. Rueben Owens II (517 rushing yards) leads the backfield.
  • Kicking. Illinois transfer David Olano started the year 16 for 16 on field goals. He has missed a few since, at South Carolina and against Tennessee. (SI, Nov. 25)

What it means: A win would end A&M's season at 5-7 and its four-game losing streak. It could also cost Texas its shot at the SEC title game. A loss means a 4-8 finish, a year after the program's first CFP appearance. For Texas, a win keeps the SEC Championship Game in play. The Longhorns get in if Alabama loses to Auburn or Tennessee loses at Vanderbilt on Saturday. They can also get in if Mississippi State or LSU loses. (AP/ESPN)
