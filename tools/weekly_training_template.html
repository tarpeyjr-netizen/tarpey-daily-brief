One row per week, newest first. A dash means nothing was logged for that lift that week. The Average row counts every week for running and rowing, zero weeks included, and only weeks with a logged set for each lift. The first week (starting Dec 29) is partial: the logs begin Jan 1.

How these are measured

Running: sum of distance (miles) and time (minutes) of runs in the 2026 Tracker Workout Log, by Monday-start week.

Rowing: Concept2 logbook weekly summaries (all machine types). Meters are converted to kilometers. Time is Concept2's total time, which includes rest in interval workouts.

Lift max: the heaviest set weight logged that week, not an estimated one-rep max. Bench is barbell flat bench, squat is barbell back squat, deadlift includes hex-bar. Overhead press combines barbell, Swiss bar and dumbbell presses: the heaviest weight that week, with dumbbells counted as the pair. Smith machine, goblet, landmine, front-squat and Romanian variations are left out. Weights are parsed from the free-text lifting log, so odd entries may be missed.
