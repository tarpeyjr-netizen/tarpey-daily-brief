Monday, November 2, 2026

🏒 Canes Blank Jets 7-0 as Kochetkov Hits 10 Straight

The Hurricanes routed the Winnipeg Jets 7-0 at Lenovo Center on Sunday, with Pyotr Kochetkov making 26 saves for his second shutout of the season and a franchise-record 10th straight win to open the year. Sean Walker scored twice, one of them shorthanded, and Juuso Välimäki, Sebastian Aho, Logan Stankoven, Mark Jankowski and Jesperi Kotkaniemi each added a goal, with Aho and Jankowski scoring on the power play. It was Carolina's fourth consecutive victory. The Hurricanes visit Nashville on Tuesday at 8 p.m. before returning home Saturday against Chicago.

🚨 Halloween Weekend Shootings Rattle Raleigh Despite Added Patrols

Two people were shot early Sunday at Bison Bar on East Whitaker Mill Road, and Raleigh police said both victims have non-life-threatening injuries and no suspects have been identified. Hours earlier, a separate Halloween-night shooting on Linville Ridge Drive in the Hedingham neighborhood sent one person to the hospital, also with injuries that were not life-threatening, and police had not released a motive. The incidents came even though the city had closed Glenwood South to traffic and boosted police presence for the holiday. In Chapel Hill, by contrast, streets stayed open as police handled Halloween like a home football night, with UNC hosting Miami at Kenan Stadium.

🚨 Man Killed in Wendell Crash Heading to Marathon

Adam Lynch died in a head-on collision near Smithfield and Bissette roads in Wendell around 5:45 a.m. Sunday while he and his wife, Maria Young, were driving to the City of Oaks Marathon in Raleigh. The State Highway Patrol said a black vehicle crossed the center line and struck their red car, and Young was hospitalized. Xavier Snead, 20, faces felony charges of death by vehicle and serious injury by vehicle, and charging documents say he was driving while impaired. The race itself went on, with thousands of runners and spectators lining Glenwood Avenue before the 7 a.m. start.

🚨 Four Teens Shot in Durham on Hedgerow Place

Durham police are investigating a shooting that left at least four young people wounded Sunday night on Hedgerow Place. Officers responding around 11:05 p.m. found three people with gunshot wounds, one with life-threatening injuries, and a fourth juvenile later arrived at a hospital with a non-life-threatening wound. No arrests or suspect information had been released. Anyone with information is asked to call Officer J. Zemites at 919-560-4582 or Durham CrimeStoppers at 919-683-1200.

🌡 Durham Drought Nears One-Year Mark With Stage 3 Possible

Severe drought persists in part of Durham County, and ABC11 reports the city's Stage 2 water restrictions, in place since June 15, could escalate to Stage 3 if conditions do not improve. November marks one year since Durham was first classified as in drought, and impacts could last through the winter. Durham Garden Center manager Keith Lukowski said reservoirs are showing more shoreline than he has seen in a long time and urged gardeners to add organic matter to hold moisture. The forecast offers little help: Election Day looks sunny with highs in the mid-70s, RDU has gone 15 days without measurable rain, and a Midwest storm may bring uncertain rain Thursday into Friday.

🏛 Early Voting Ends With Over 2 Million Ballots Cast

North Carolina voters had cast more than 2 million ballots as of Friday, just over 23% of statewide turnout, as early voting closed Saturday. Early voting is running 36% ahead of the same point in 2020, while absentee voting has declined, and election officials say turnout typically rises at the start and end of the early period. Raleigh voters interviewed cited the economy, inflation, taxes and health care as top concerns. Polls open Tuesday for the general election.

Sunday, November 1, 2026

🏈 NC State Routs Cal in First Game After Doeren

NC State beat California 31-10 at Carter-Finley Stadium on Homecoming Saturday, giving interim coach Todd Goebbel his first win and lifting the Wolfpack to 4-4 (2-3 ACC). Quarterback C.J. Bailey threw for 192 yards and two touchdowns, including a 38-yard score to Chance Robinson, and Davion Gause ran for 104 yards and a touchdown. Cal tied the game 10-10 at halftime before NC State pulled away in the third quarter, and players gave Goebbel the game ball. He said the win was for fired coach Dave Doeren, who was dismissed Oct. 24 after a loss at Stanford. Meanwhile, Philip Rivers says nothing is imminent on a possible return to coach the Wolfpack.

🚨 Glenwood South Shuts Down for Halloween Night

Raleigh closed Glenwood Avenue South to everything but pedestrians on Saturday night, with barricades going up at 5 p.m. between Hillsborough and Peace streets and police command centers and extra patrols in place. Crowds and long lines had formed by mid-afternoon, and some attendees welcomed the added security. The measures follow July Fourth violence on Glenwood, though the Wake County sheriff said his office had found no credible Halloween takeover threats. With revelers expected to clear out around 3 a.m., crews had only a few hours before the City of Oaks Marathon starts at 7 a.m. Sunday.

🏗 Cary Lines Up Winery, Steam Pots and South Indian Dining

Cooper's Hawk Winery & Restaurants will open its first Triangle location on Nov. 9 at Parkside Town Commons in Cary, according to WRAL's Foodie News roundup. The restaurant offers a wine list of more than 60 varietals chosen by Master Sommelier Emily Wines, plus a heated covered patio and two private dining rooms. Also in Cary, Bar O Bar, a South Indian restaurant from Michelin-trained Chef Vikash Vumudi, is expected in December at 2301 Corley Road. Topsail Steamer, a take-home seafood steam-pot concept, is targeting spring 2027 for 214 E. Chatham St.

🏒 Canes Host Jets Seeking Tenth Straight for Kochetkov

The Hurricanes return to Lenovo Center on Sunday at 1 p.m. against the Winnipeg Jets, with goalie Pyotr Kochetkov looking to extend a franchise-record nine-game winning streak to open the season. Carolina enters at 11-2-2 after a 3-2 win over Columbus on Thursday that stretched its point streak to five games. The game was listed on the schedule for Nov. 1, and no result was available at the time of this edition.

Saturday, October 31, 2026

🏒 Kochetkov Wins Ninth Straight to Open Season

The Hurricanes held off the Columbus Blue Jackets 3-2 at Lenovo Center on Thursday, with Pyotr Kochetkov making 24 saves to extend his season-opening winning streak to nine games, a franchise record. Jordan Martinook, Logan Stankoven and Mark Jankowski scored for Carolina, while Seth Jarvis added two assists. Columbus scored both of its goals in the third period, but the Canes closed it out to move to 11-2-2 and stretch their point streak to five games, per the ESPN game summary.

🏛 Durham DA Drops Charges in Abortion Arrest Case

Durham County District Attorney Satana Deberry dropped all charges against Syeisha Johnson, 18, and Elijah Speight, 20, nearly three months after their arrest in an alleged attempted third-trimester medication abortion. Deberry cited legal technicalities around fetal personhood and insufficient evidence that the medication caused the preterm delivery. The decision was reported in the News & Observer's Wednesday headlines roundup.

🎓 NC State Fraternity Suspended Amid Hazing Probe

NC State's Sigma Nu chapter is on interim suspension while university police investigate an anonymous hazing report. The national organization says it is cooperating with the investigation and called hazing "the antithesis of the fraternity's mission." ABC11 separately reported that a fraternity house was searched as part of a criminal investigation into hazing allegations.

🏗 Durham Taproom Closes as Five Guys Eyes Fayetteville Street

Pour Taproom, the Durham self-serve beer bar that brought pay-by-the-ounce taps to the city in 2017, closes on Halloween, with co-owner Andrew Shaver blaming steadily falling revenue since late 2021. In Raleigh, Five Guys has filed sketch plans for 442 Fayetteville St. in City Plaza, including interior renovations and a new rear space for a walk-in cooler and storage. City review is scheduled for Nov. 4.

🚨 Durham Neighbors Hold Vigil for Slain Mother

Residents of Durham's Parkwood neighborhood gathered Thursday evening for a candlelight vigil honoring Victoria James, a mother of two shot and killed on Revere Road in September. Pastor Ben Krey led prayers alongside other local clergy, and neighbors described a normally quiet street. Advocates noted that the North Carolina Coalition Against Domestic Violence counted 53 domestic violence-related homicides statewide from January through September.

Friday, October 30, 2026

🏒 Kochetkov and Canes Keep Perfect Start Rolling

The Hurricanes held off the Columbus Blue Jackets at home, with NHL.com's recap crediting goalie Pyotr Kochetkov with becoming the first goaltender in franchise history to open a season with nine straight wins. He had matched the club's mark of eight on Oct. 27, when Carolina beat Boston 2-1. The team is next scheduled at home on Sunday, Nov. 1, at 1 p.m. against the Winnipeg Jets.

🤝 Raleigh Braces for Homecoming, Halloween and Marathon Weekend

Raleigh expects one of its busiest weekends of the year, with NC State's noon Homecoming game against Cal on Saturday, Halloween revelry downtown that night and the City of Oaks Marathon on Sunday morning. A stretch of Glenwood South between Jones and Peace streets will close at 7 p.m. Saturday, and police are adding officers there and in Oakwood and along Fayetteville Street after a July Fourth crowd of about 5,000 brought shootings and fights. Meanwhile, the Oakwood "Halloween House" will go on after the city required homeowner Jesse Jones to obtain a special event permit for a crowd expected to top 5,000 and close five blocks. Jones secured insurance, off-duty officers and a cleanup plan to satisfy the city.

🎓 NC State Sigma Nu Hazing Probe Likely to Bring Charges

Wake County District Attorney Lorrin Freeman told WRAL she expects criminal charges from the investigation into NC State's Sigma Nu fraternity. The chapter is on interim suspension and NC State police are leading the criminal probe, with Freeman's office in contact. She said it is too early to discuss specific charges, though a former federal prosecutor noted hazing can be charged as assault, potentially a felony depending on severity.

🌡 Durham Warns Water Limits Edging Toward Stage 3

Durham officials say Stage 2 water restrictions, in place since June 15, are getting closer to Stage 3 and are expected to last through the winter. Sprinklers and hose-end cleaning of driveways, sidewalks and buildings are prohibited, and more than 400 violations have been reported without any fines so far. Raleigh reports Falls Lake is 67% full and would need to fall to 40% before its own restrictions begin.

🎓 Wake School Board Weighs Fewer Student Laptops

Wake County's school board heard from students and principals on Oct. 27 as it rethinks one-device-per-student purchasing. Students cited eye strain, glitches and distraction, while principals said computers are required for state testing and give some low-income students access they would otherwise lack. With pandemic relief funds exhausted, the board may buy fewer devices, such as half as many Chromebooks in lower grades, and district staff will present a plan in December.

🏛 Triangle Ballots: Bonds, Not Sales Taxes

With Election Day Tuesday, WUNC reports 11 counties will vote on sales tax increases, but none are in the Triangle. Wake County instead has a school construction bond, and Raleigh is seeking bonds for affordable housing and transportation. Statewide, constitutional amendments limiting property and income tax increases also appear on the ballot.
