Saturday, October 3, 2026

🏒 Capitals Rout Hurricanes in Home Opener Weekend

Alex Tuch scored twice in his Washington debut as the Capitals beat the Hurricanes 5-2 on Friday night, building a three-goal first period lead. Sebastian Aho and Logan Stankoven scored for Carolina, and Brandon Bussi stopped 22 shots. Alex Ovechkin, 41, opened his 22nd NHL season with an assist on Tuch's first goal. The loss drops the Hurricanes to 0-1-1 after Tuesday's overtime defeat to Florida, with a trip to Philadelphia on tap Saturday.

🚨 Durham Police Clear Fayetteville Street Encampment

Durham police say a homeless encampment along the American Tobacco Trail is gone after "Operation Safe Passage," which Chief Stephanie Mardis authorized after a spike in resident complaints. Officers arrested two men Tuesday and seized two handguns, more than $9,000 in cash and over 30 grams of crack cocaine near Fayetteville and East Pilot streets. Police cited more than 700 calls for service in the area, including two homicides, and residents noted tents sat very close to an elementary school. All but one person left voluntarily, and the last person received a trespass notice and also left.

🎓 NC State Opens Battery Makerspace on Centennial Campus

NC State has opened a Battery and Energy Storage Technology Makerspace aimed at training students for one of the state's fastest-growing industries. Students and faculty can design, test and improve battery technologies there, and two lithium-ion laboratory classes are already under way. Toyota, which runs a battery manufacturing plant of nearly 7 million square feet in Randolph County, plans to use the space for training and research, according to ABC11.

🎓 Duke Philosopher Wins MacArthur Genius Grant

Duke cognitive scientist and philosopher Felipe De Brigard is one of 20 2026 MacArthur Fellows, earning an $800,000 grant. De Brigard, 47, has been at Duke since 2013 and studies how flexible human memory is and how emotions shape it. His work includes the "Memoria & Perdón" project in Colombia, which examined how forgiveness can help victims of political violence. He is the fifth Duke scholar to receive the award, per Axios Raleigh.

Friday, October 2, 2026

🏒 Panthers Spoil Hurricanes' Banner Night in Overtime

Gustav Forsling scored with 4.3 seconds left in overtime Tuesday to give the Florida Panthers a 1-0 win over the Hurricanes in the season opener at Lenovo Center. The result came on the night Carolina raised its 2026 Stanley Cup banner, with captain Jordan Staal carrying the Cup around the ice before the Canes performed their pregame Storm Surge. Goalie Brandon Bussi, who started the final three games of the Cup Final, made 19 saves in the loss, according to WRAL. The defending champions get a chance to regroup right away, with a road game in Washington on Friday and another in Philadelphia on Saturday.

🏛 Wake County Leaders Warn Growth Is Straining Budget

Wake County Board of Commissioners Chair Don Mial used the 2026 State of the County address to say that housing remains the county's biggest challenge as growth and state funding gaps squeeze the budget. "We have been standing in the gap for years, but it's getting a lot harder," Mial said, calling this a tough budget year. The county is adding about 24,000 residents a year, or 66 a day, and its population is approaching 1.2 million, per ABC11. Wake County Public Schools will receive an additional $25.3 million in county operating money this fiscal year, bringing total county operating support to $768 million.

🏗 Downtown Raleigh's Round Landmark Reopens as Hotel Indigo

The former Holiday Inn tower in downtown Raleigh has reopened as Hotel Indigo after a $36 million renovation. The 204-room hotel was built in 1969, closed in 2025, and was gutted and redesigned inside while keeping its distinctive circular exterior. A top-floor bar and restaurant called High Ground Lounge pairs skyline views with Southern cuisine, according to Lodging. Backers say the project is aimed in part at attracting larger conventions and events to the city.

🚨 Suspect Charged After Chapel Hill and Raleigh Gas Station Robberies

Raleigh police arrested 24-year-old Alagi Isaasik Jagne on Wednesday after an incident at a Refuel on Glenwood Avenue, and Chapel Hill police say he is the suspect in an earlier armed robbery at a Circle K on Estes Drive. A firearm was recovered, no injuries were reported in either case, and Jagne faces charges including robbery with a dangerous weapon, second-degree kidnapping and assault with a deadly weapon. The Chapel Hill robbery, reported just before noon, prompted Estes Hills Elementary and Phillips Middle School to enter secure mode for roughly 90 minutes, as the town's police notice described the suspect in a scream mask and black hoodie.

🏀 UNC-Duke Basketball Returns to Broadcast TV

For the first time in more than 15 years, the North Carolina-Duke men's basketball rivalry is returning to broadcast television, with the season's first meeting set to air on ABC on February 13. The announcement comes as the ACC's 2026-27 schedule has NC State hosting neither the Tar Heels nor the Blue Devils in Raleigh, a first in more than a century, according to CBS17.

Thursday, October 1, 2026

🏒 Hurricanes Open Season with Overtime Loss to Florida

The Carolina Hurricanes began their 2026-27 season Tuesday with a 1-0 overtime loss to the Florida Panthers, leaving them 0-0-1 and still atop the Metropolitan Division on the strength of the single standings point, according to ESPN's schedule and standings. The opener is the first of an 84-game slate for a team coming off a Stanley Cup Final win over Vegas that ended a 20-year title drought. Carolina turns around quickly, with games at Washington on October 2 and Philadelphia on October 3.

🚨 Man Dies in Downtown Durham Bar Shooting

A man died after a shooting at a downtown Durham bar, WRAL reported. Details on the investigation were limited in the available coverage, so readers should check WRAL for the latest from Durham police. The incident comes as Durham County reports a 4% drop in its "Top 8 Crimes" for the year to date, a decline CBS17 has tracked in the sheriff's office figures.

🏛 Durham Commissioners Mark Hospital Anniversary, Seek Sheriff Advisers

Durham County commissioners met Monday and proclaimed October 3 Duke Regional Hospital 50th Anniversary Recognition Day. The Sheriff's Office is also accepting applications for its Community Advisory Board through October 9. Looking ahead, early voting for the November general election in Durham County runs October 15 through October 31, and the Move-A-Bull City open streets event closes 1.2 miles of Blackwell, Corcoran and Foster Streets to cars on October 4.

🤝 Chapel Hill Lands $10 Million Gift for Cedar Falls Park

A $10 million gift to the Town of Chapel Hill will pay for a new building at Cedar Falls Park and two greenway extensions, Chapelboro reports. The Town Council also approved a $65 out-of-town public library fee. Elsewhere, Tongde Wu has taken the oath as the newest Chapel Hill-Carrboro school board member, appointed to finish Rani Dasi's term, and county commissioners are set to meet jointly with the city and Orange County school boards on student behavioral health and future budgets.

🏗 Former Holiday Inn Reopens as Hotel Indigo in Raleigh

The former Holiday Inn reopens Thursday, October 1, as Hotel Indigo after a $36 million renovation, timed for NC State's Parents and Families Weekend, according to DoRaleigh. The hotel includes High Ground Lounge, a top-floor cocktail bar with city views. In Fuquay-Varina, construction is underway on the first phase of Gold Leaf Crossing, a 106-acre shopping center planned for 800,000 square feet of retail with Target among future tenants.

🏀 ACC Schedule Keeps UNC and Duke Out of Raleigh

The ACC's 2026-27 men's basketball schedule means NC State will host neither UNC nor Duke, the first time that has happened in more than a century, CBS17 reported. On the volleyball court, NC State drew its first-ever sellout, 5,500 fans, for its ACC opener but fell in four sets to 24th-ranked UNC, per NC State Athletics.

Wednesday, September 30, 2026

🏒 Panthers Spoil Canes' Banner Night in Overtime

Gustav Forsling's one-timer with 4.3 seconds left in overtime gave the Florida Panthers a 1-0 win over the Hurricanes on Tuesday night at Lenovo Center, just after Carolina raised its 2026 Stanley Cup banner before a sellout crowd. Goalie Brandon Bussi stopped 19 shots and kept the game scoreless into overtime, while Florida's Jacob Markstrom made 15 saves in his return to the Panthers' net. The teams combined for 13 power plays and neither scored, according to NHL.com's recap. Coach Rod Brind'Amour said afterward that Bussi was the reason the Canes earned a point, and WRAL's coverage notes Carolina played without Seth Jarvis, who is on injured reserve after offseason shoulder surgery.

🏛 Durham Commissioners Meet as Medicaid Changes Near

The Durham County Board of Commissioners met Monday and approved a budget amendment recognizing $35,000 from the City of Durham for food and nutrition support, along with an interlocal cooperation agreement between the county and the city, according to the county's session highlights. Commissioners also passed a resolution recognizing September 26 as Prince Hall Day in Durham County. Separately, Durham County Social Services says new federal Medicaid rules begin taking effect Thursday, October 1, with more changes on January 1, 2027, and it is available to answer residents' questions. The first round narrows eligibility for many noncitizens, and a work and community engagement requirement for most adults ages 19 to 64 follows in January, as WUNC has explained.
