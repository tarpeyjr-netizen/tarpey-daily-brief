🏛 Wake DA Nears Decision in Cary Spending Probe

Wake County District Attorney Lorrin Freeman says her office is "30 to 60 days away" from wrapping up its criminal investigation into spending by former Town of Cary employees, WRAL reports. The probe centers on former town manager Sean Stegall's use of the town credit card. A 2,600-page State Auditor's report released in July flagged tens of thousands of dollars in purchases without a clear government purpose, including high-cost meals, alcohol, travel and retreat spending. Freeman said in September that the case had stalled while she waited on documents from the auditor's office. Stegall, who was placed on leave in November 2025 and resigned a month later, has not responded to requests for comment.

🚨 Garner Man Charged With Threatening Wake DA

Raleigh police have charged Demetrious Hannan, 36, of Garner, with threatening a public official after court documents say he threatened in a Facebook video to kill District Attorney Lorrin Freeman, according to WRAL. Police cited earlier threats as well, including one referencing firearms, and said they believed he was likely to carry it out. Hannan was already out on bond for trespassing at a county office inside the Wake County Justice Center. He is being held on a $150,000 bond and is due in court Dec. 14. Freeman declined to comment; Wiley Nickel succeeds her as district attorney in 2027.

🚨 Chapel Hill Robbery Suspect Caught After Durham Chase

Durham police arrested Shyree McCrea, 25, a Chapel Hill robbery suspect with multiple outstanding warrants, after a chase on Tuesday afternoon, ABC11 reports. Officers from the department's Special Operations Unit tried to stop his car near E. Main Street and Alston Avenue just after 2:30 p.m., but it fled. Bystanders pointed officers to an apartment complex on Old Chapel Hill Road, where McCrea was found in a nearby unit and arrested without incident. He faces charges including robbery with a dangerous weapon, first-degree kidnapping and assault by strangulation. Police Chief Stephanie Mardis said she remains "committed to the apprehension of violent criminals who commit gun crimes in the City of Durham."

🤝 Wake at Home Houses Encampment Residents Before Freeze

After closing a South Raleigh encampment near a busy road this month, the Wake at Home initiative says 29 former residents are now in safe housing or soon will be, WUNC reports. Continuum of Care lead agency director Eileen Rosa said the work takes six to eight weeks of consistent outreach, and that most people approached accept housing offers. The effort has $22 million in established funding plus a $12 million housing bond Raleigh voters approved in the midterm. Wake County is in its second White Flag shelter activation of the season, which opens extra beds when temperatures drop to 35 degrees or below. The initiative aims to move 400 people out of encampments by December 2027.

🏒 Aho Becomes Canes' All-Time Goal Leader

Sebastian Aho scored his 323rd career goal in Saturday's win over Edmonton, passing Eric Staal for the most goals by a Hurricanes player since the franchise moved to North Carolina, the team reports. The power-play goal was the game-winner, and Aho called Staal "royalty" and "a legend of a player." Carolina followed with a 5-2 win over the Rangers on Sunday to complete a winning weekend. At 18-3-3 and first in the Metropolitan Division, the Canes visit Tampa Bay tonight at 7 p.m. seeking a third straight win, per the team's preview, with Nikolaj Ehlers expected to miss a second game.

🎓 Durham Music Teacher's Classroom Videos Go Viral

Tim Lorelli, the music teacher at R.N. Harris Elementary School in Durham, has built an Instagram following of more than 57,000 with videos of his students performing everything from Kendrick Lamar to Dolly Parton, WUNC reports. Artists including Erykah Badu and Tyler, The Creator have praised the clips. Lorelli, a Chapel Hill High graduate who studied jazz at UNC-Chapel Hill, spent about 15 years teaching in Brooklyn before returning to the Triangle and has been at R.N. Harris for six years. He pairs songs with history lessons, but says "90% of the stuff that happens in my classroom isn't recorded."
