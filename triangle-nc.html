🏒 Canes Rally Past Lightning, Host Ottawa Tonight

The Hurricanes scored three times in the third period to beat Tampa Bay 3-1 on Wednesday night, with Eric Robinson tying the game, Jaccob Slavin scoring his first goal of the season less than two minutes later and Jackson Blake adding an empty-netter. Brandon Bussi made a season-high 32 saves for his fourth straight win. At 19-3-3, Carolina joins the 2001-02 Red Wings and 1983-84 Oilers as the only NHL teams to reach 19 wins before Thanksgiving. The Canes are back at Lenovo Center tonight at 7 p.m. against Atlantic Division leader Ottawa (17-7-3), going for a nine-game point streak and wearing their Hometown Remix uniforms, according to the team's preview.

🏈 Bowl Eligibility at Stake in UNC–NC State Rivalry

North Carolina and NC State close their regular seasons today at noon on ACC Network, and both teams come in at 5-6, so the winner is bowl eligible, WRAL reports. NC State has won five straight in the series, which ties the Wolfpack's longest run in a rivalry dating to 1894, and no current Tar Heel has beaten NC State. Bill Belichick's UNC team has lost four of its last five after a 4-2 start. NC State is playing under interim coach Todd Goebbel, with Philip Rivers hired but not taking over until next season. On the hardwood, the UNC men's basketball team fell 72-59 to West Virginia Friday night in Charlotte and dropped to 5-2.

🚨 Deadly Thanksgiving Night in Raleigh and Durham

A Raleigh driver is charged with DWI, felony hit and run and felony death by motor vehicle after 49-year-old Miguel Sanchez was hit and killed on Spring Forest Road just west of Capital Boulevard shortly before 6:30 p.m. Thursday, ABC11 reports. Police say Christine Montella, 34, was driving east in a BMW X5, left the scene and later called 911 to say she had struck something. About four and a half hours later in Durham, two men were shot around 11 p.m. at an apartment complex in the 200 block of South Alston Avenue, and one of them died at the hospital, according to WRAL. The second man's injuries were not life-threatening, and Durham police have not released suspect information.

🌡 Thanksgiving Storm Helps the Drought, but Not Much

A coastal storm this week dropped several inches of rain on parts of Wake and Durham counties, and Falls Lake rose about a foot between Wednesday and Friday evening, WRAL reports. Parts of both counties are still in extreme drought, though. Raleigh says its remaining Falls Lake water supply pool is at 59 percent. Durham's Lake Michie is still nearly 14 feet below capacity, and the city, which had 74 days of easily accessible water as of November 24, remains under Stage 2 mandatory restrictions. WRAL meteorologist Elizabeth Gardner said the region still needs 10 to 12 inches of rain within a month to catch up.

🏛 Gas Tax Returns as New NC Laws Hit Tuesday

A batch of new state laws takes effect Tuesday, December 1, and the state gas tax, suspended since mid-October, is set to resume unless lawmakers extend the suspension, WRAL reports. Under Senate Bill 445, people will be able to sue robocallers for $10,000 per call. Riders on the fastest class of e-bikes must wear helmets, and cities and counties may adopt stricter e-bike rules. Littering fines rise sharply, and the new budget raises fees for divorces, lawsuits and getting a license back after a drunk-driving case. Other bills, on constitutional carry and election law among others, could take effect only if the legislature overrides Gov. Josh Stein's vetoes.

🏗 Black Friday Jams Southpoint; Cary Steps Up Patrols

Black Friday crowds packed The Streets at Southpoint in Durham and backed traffic up onto I-40 and the Fayetteville Road exit ramp by late afternoon, with heavy traffic also reported at Raleigh's Crabtree Valley Mall, WRAL reports. Cary police say they are adding patrols around the town's busiest shopping centers for the holiday season to deter theft. They are also working with retailers' loss-prevention teams and urging shoppers to lock their cars and keep purchases out of sight. Earlier Friday, falling trees and branches knocked out power to about 1,725 Duke Energy customers in eastern Cary and western Raleigh, and service returned for many by 11:15 a.m.
