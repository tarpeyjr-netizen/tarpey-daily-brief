Tuesday, October 6, 2026

🏛 Chapel Hill Weighs Fire Station and 105 Affordable Homes

The Chapel Hill Town Council meets at 6 p.m. Wednesday to consider a proposal for a new fire station paired with 105 affordable housing units on Weaver Dairy Road Extension, along with a plan to add 12 housing units at the Cedars in Meadowmont. Across the county line, Orange County commissioners meet tonight to hear final audit results on the contested property revaluation and an update on an ordinance to regulate data centers. Carrboro's council, also meeting tonight, will consider a CompostNow curbside composting service that could be the first town-wide program of its kind in North Carolina.

🏛 Raleigh Launches Housing Production Dashboard

Raleigh has put its new affordable housing dashboard online for residents after presenting it to the city council. The interactive map shows completed city-funded projects, pipeline developments, public housing and tax-credit sites, and tracks how 2020 Affordable Housing Bond money has been committed. The city says it has created or preserved roughly 5,800 units over the last decade, with drilldowns by council district and fiscal year.

🚨 Raleigh Police Chase Car Break-In Suspects; Club Alive Arrest

Raleigh police took one person into custody and are still looking for a second after reports of people breaking into vehicles around 2 a.m. Monday on Schenley Drive in south Raleigh, WRAL reported. Nearly a dozen police units responded, and one suspect was caught near Garner Road. Separately, detectives arrested 33-year-old Dontayvious Devonte Cotton on Oct. 1 in the Aug. 23 shooting at Club Alive on Fayetteville Street, and he faces four counts of assault with a deadly weapon with intent to kill inflicting serious injury. Police said a fight inside the club escalated before four people were shot, all of whom were expected to survive.

🏈 NC State Tops Louisville as Duke Rests at 4-0

NC State beat Louisville 31-28 at home to improve to 3-2 overall and 1-1 in the ACC, with three defensive turnovers helping quarterback CJ Bailey and the Wolfpack hold on. UNC fell to 2-2 after its 37-26 loss to No. 3 Notre Dame, and Duke, unbeaten at 4-0, had an open week before it heads to Georgia Tech on Saturday. The Tar Heels and Blue Devils then meet Oct. 17 in Durham, with kickoff set for 3:30 p.m. on ESPN2.

🏒 Hurricanes Visit Montreal After Overtime Win in Philadelphia

Carolina won 3-2 in overtime at Philadelphia on Saturday behind goaltender Pyotr Kochetkov, bouncing back from a 5-2 loss to Washington that coach Rod Brind'Amour said left his team outmatched. The Hurricanes sit at 1-1-1 and visit the Canadiens tonight, per the ESPN schedule. Forward Seth Jarvis remains out following offseason shoulder surgery, with a return projected between late October and late December.

Monday, October 5, 2026

🏒 Aho Sparks Hurricanes to First Win in Overtime

Sebastian Aho scored twice and set up Shayne Gostisbehere's power-play winner as the Hurricanes beat the Flyers 3-2 in overtime Saturday night for their first victory of the season. Aho scored on the opening shift of the game and again on the power play in the third period to tie it, and Gostisbehere ended it with 2:09 left in the extra session. The Stanley Cup champions had opened 2026-27 with an overtime loss to Florida and a 5-2 home loss to Washington on Friday. The win extended Carolina's streak of road-opener victories to nine seasons, while forward Seth Jarvis remains out with a shoulder injury expected to keep him sidelined until around December.

🏈 Notre Dame Outlasts UNC 37-26 in the Rain

Third-ranked Notre Dame scored 16 straight points across the third and fourth quarters to beat North Carolina 37-26 on Saturday in Chapel Hill. The Irish improved to 5-0 while the Tar Heels fell to 2-2. Next up for UNC is a home date with Duke on Oct. 17, the first of several Triangle rivalry games on the ACC calendar this month, according to CBS17.

🚨 Fatal Shooting and Highway Crash Mark Durham Sunday

One man died and another was wounded in a shooting around 12:30 a.m. Sunday in the 4400 block of Durham-Chapel Hill Boulevard, WRAL reported. The survivor's injuries were not life-threatening, no suspects have been arrested, and investigators are asking for tips through Durham Crimestoppers at 919-683-1200. Separately, a four-car wreck Sunday morning forced the closure of eastbound Interstate 40 at NC 55 in Durham and caused miles of gridlock before lanes reopened.

🚨 Arrest Follows Gunfire on West Franklin Street

Chapel Hill police arrested a suspect after shots were fired around 7 p.m. Friday in the 100 block of West Franklin Street, according to an Alert Carolina notice issued at 8:54 p.m. No injuries were reported, and the university said there was no ongoing threat to the campus community. The incident occurred off campus and the police investigation remained open at the time of the alert.

🏗 Cary's Veloxis Folded Into New Asahi Kasei Unit

Cary-based Veloxis Pharmaceuticals will be integrated into a new global commercial organization called Asahi Kasei Therapeutics, according to BioBuzz. Veloxis has grown about 20 percent a year since 2020 on its kidney transplant drug Envarsus XR, and parent company Asahi Kasei is positioning it as an anchor for western operations as it targets 300 billion yen in sales by 2030. In Durham, Humacyte added Scott Coward and Paul Kuznik to its board as it prepares to roll out its engineered vessel for vascular access, and Atsena Therapeutics reported that foveal schisis resolution persisted in seven of nine treated eyes in its gene therapy trial.

Sunday, October 4, 2026

🏒 Capitals Rout Hurricanes on Banner-Year Opening Night

Alex Tuch scored twice in his Washington debut as the Capitals built a 4-0 lead and beat the Stanley Cup champion Carolina Hurricanes 5-2 on Friday night. Sebastian Aho scored on a power play late in the second period and Logan Stankoven added a goal in the third, but Carolina never recovered from the early deficit. The loss follows Tuesday's 1-0 overtime defeat to Florida, leaving the Hurricanes 0-1-1 to open the season, and the local North State Journal wrote that the team lacked urgency. Carolina visited the Philadelphia Flyers on Saturday.

🏈 Notre Dame Pulls Away From UNC, 37-26

Third-ranked Notre Dame beat North Carolina 37-26 in Chapel Hill on Saturday, with quarterback CJ Carr throwing four touchdown passes and running for another. The Irish remained unbeaten, while the Tar Heels dropped a marquee Week 5 home game. A recap from NCFootballNews has more on how the game unfolded.

🤝 Move-A-Bull City Opens Durham Streets Sunday

Durham's largest open streets celebration, Move-A-Bull City, runs Sunday from noon to 4 p.m. with 1.2 miles of Blackwell, Corcoran and Foster streets closed to cars. More than 160 activity partners are expected to offer arts, music, food, wellness and local government booths along the route. The free event is organized by Bike Durham, the City of Durham and the Durham County Public Health Department's Partnership for a Healthy Durham, and is among the weekend picks from WRAL.

Saturday, October 3, 2026

🏒 Capitals Rout Hurricanes in Home Opener Weekend

Alex Tuch scored twice in his Washington debut as the Capitals beat the Hurricanes 5-2 on Friday night, building a three-goal first period lead. Sebastian Aho and Logan Stankoven scored for Carolina, and Brandon Bussi stopped 22 shots. Alex Ovechkin, 41, opened his 22nd NHL season with an assist on Tuch's first goal. The loss drops the Hurricanes to 0-1-1 after Tuesday's overtime defeat to Florida, with a trip to Philadelphia on tap Saturday.

🚨 Durham Police Clear Fayetteville Street Encampment

Durham police say a homeless encampment along the American Tobacco Trail is gone after "Operation Safe Passage," which Chief Stephanie Mardis authorized after a spike in resident complaints. Officers arrested two men Tuesday and seized two handguns, more than $9,000 in cash and over 30 grams of crack cocaine near Fayetteville and East Pilot streets. Police cited more than 700 calls for service in the area, including two homicides, and residents noted tents sat very close to an elementary school. All but one person left voluntarily, and the last person received a trespass notice and also left.

🎓 NC State Opens Battery Makerspace on Centennial Campus

NC State has opened a Battery and Energy Storage Technology Makerspace aimed at training students for one of the state's fastest-growing industries. Students and faculty can design, test and improve battery technologies there, and two lithium-ion laboratory classes are already under way. Toyota, which runs a battery manufacturing plant of nearly 7 million square feet in Randolph County, plans to use the space for training and research, according to ABC11.

🎓 Duke Philosopher Wins MacArthur Genius Grant

Duke cognitive scientist and philosopher Felipe De Brigard is one of 20 2026 MacArthur Fellows, earning an $800,000 grant. De Brigard, 47, has been at Duke since 2013 and studies how flexible human memory is and how emotions shape it. His work includes the "Memoria & Perdón" project in Colombia, which examined how forgiveness can help victims of political violence. He is the fifth Duke scholar to receive the award, per Axios Raleigh.
