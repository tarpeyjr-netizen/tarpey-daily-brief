Friday, October 30, 2026

🏒 Kochetkov and Canes Keep Perfect Start Rolling

The Hurricanes held off the Columbus Blue Jackets at home, with NHL.com's recap crediting goalie Pyotr Kochetkov with becoming the first goaltender in franchise history to open a season with nine straight wins. He had matched the club's mark of eight on Oct. 27, when Carolina beat Boston 2-1. The team is next scheduled at home on Sunday, Nov. 1, at 1 p.m. against the Winnipeg Jets.

🤝 Raleigh Braces for Homecoming, Halloween and Marathon Weekend

Raleigh expects one of its busiest weekends of the year, with NC State's noon Homecoming game against Cal on Saturday, Halloween revelry downtown that night and the City of Oaks Marathon on Sunday morning. A stretch of Glenwood South between Jones and Peace streets will close at 7 p.m. Saturday, and police are adding officers there and in Oakwood and along Fayetteville Street after a July Fourth crowd of about 5,000 brought shootings and fights. Meanwhile, the Oakwood "Halloween House" will go on after the city required homeowner Jesse Jones to obtain a special event permit for a crowd expected to top 5,000 and close five blocks. Jones secured insurance, off-duty officers and a cleanup plan to satisfy the city.

🎓 NC State Sigma Nu Hazing Probe Likely to Bring Charges

Wake County District Attorney Lorrin Freeman told WRAL she expects criminal charges from the investigation into NC State's Sigma Nu fraternity. The chapter is on interim suspension and NC State police are leading the criminal probe, with Freeman's office in contact. She said it is too early to discuss specific charges, though a former federal prosecutor noted hazing can be charged as assault, potentially a felony depending on severity.

🌡 Durham Warns Water Limits Edging Toward Stage 3

Durham officials say Stage 2 water restrictions, in place since June 15, are getting closer to Stage 3 and are expected to last through the winter. Sprinklers and hose-end cleaning of driveways, sidewalks and buildings are prohibited, and more than 400 violations have been reported without any fines so far. Raleigh reports Falls Lake is 67% full and would need to fall to 40% before its own restrictions begin.

🎓 Wake School Board Weighs Fewer Student Laptops

Wake County's school board heard from students and principals on Oct. 27 as it rethinks one-device-per-student purchasing. Students cited eye strain, glitches and distraction, while principals said computers are required for state testing and give some low-income students access they would otherwise lack. With pandemic relief funds exhausted, the board may buy fewer devices, such as half as many Chromebooks in lower grades, and district staff will present a plan in December.

🏛 Triangle Ballots: Bonds, Not Sales Taxes

With Election Day Tuesday, WUNC reports 11 counties will vote on sales tax increases, but none are in the Triangle. Wake County instead has a school construction bond, and Raleigh is seeking bonds for affordable housing and transportation. Statewide, constitutional amendments limiting property and income tax increases also appear on the ballot.

🚨 Raleigh Woman Held Without Bond in Girlfriend's Shooting

Raleigh police arrested 18-year-old Shaliah Battista on a murder charge after she allegedly shot 20-year-old Littlemaria Garcia at a Garner Road apartment complex in South Raleigh, and a judge ordered her held without bond on Wednesday. WRAL reports that officers responded around 3:10 p.m. Tuesday, found Garcia shot, and that she later died at a hospital. Police said the two were in a dating relationship and that Battista stayed at the scene. Garcia's mother described the relationship as unhealthy and said she had struggled to get help for her daughter, while court records show Battista was arrested in Johnston County earlier this month on a concealed gun charge and posted bond.

🏒 Kochetkov Wins Eighth Straight as Hurricanes Top Bruins

The Carolina Hurricanes beat the Boston Bruins 2-1 at Lenovo Center on Tuesday, with Sebastian Aho scoring the winner at 10:19 of the third period. NHL.com's recap notes that goalie Pyotr Kochetkov won his eighth straight start to open the season, and Carolina (10-2-2) extended its point streak to four games. Jackson Blake tied the game late in the first period after Boston's Pavel Zacha scored on a power play, and the Hurricanes killed two late penalties to snap Boston's eight-game point streak. Coach Rod Brind'Amour said Taylor Hall left the morning skate with an injury and will be out for a while. Carolina hosts the Blue Jackets tonight.

🤝 Glenwood South Closure Planned for Halloween and Marathon

Raleigh police will fully close part of Glenwood South, from Jones Street to Peace Street, on Halloween night, the first such closure in recent years. ABC11 reports that parking ends at 4 p.m. Saturday, and the State Highway Patrol, the SBI and neighboring agencies will help boost the police presence downtown. Revelers must clear out around 3 a.m. Sunday, leaving crews only a few hours to clean up before the City of Oaks Marathon starts at 7 a.m. WRAL adds that Glenwood Avenue will stay closed until the marathon finishes. City officials expect very heavy traffic, with NC State's homecoming weekend adding to the crowds, and urge people to use rideshare.

Wednesday, October 28, 2026

🚨 Jury Convicts Man in 2012 Faith Hedgepeth Killing

A jury has convicted Miguel Salguero Olivares of murdering UNC Chapel Hill student Faith Hedgepeth, closing a case that took nearly 14 years to resolve. Hedgepeth was killed in her off-campus Chapel Hill apartment in 2012, and prosecutors leaned on DNA and fingerprint evidence along with graphic crime scene material. The defense argued reasonable doubt, pointing to investigative errors such as an unswabbed doorknob and misplaced evidence, and rested without calling witnesses. Jurors told ABC11 that some of them initially doubted the state had proven its case before deliberations that they described as intense and respectful led to a conviction.

🏛 Carrboro Weighs Merging Police With Chapel Hill

Carrboro is exploring a merger of its police department with the Chapel Hill Police Department, a change that would require approval from both town councils. ABC11 reports the idea follows the September retirement of Carrboro's police chief, budget pressures, and costly building renovations, along with vehicle damage from Tropical Storm Chantal that FEMA will not replace. Under the proposal, Carrboro officers would move to Chapel Hill's Millhouse Road headquarters in groups of five over four months. Town Manager Patrice Toney framed it as a chance to create efficiencies, while residents are asking for specific cost and staffing figures. A community conversation is set for Thursday and the Carrboro Town Council holds a work session on Nov. 10.

🏗 Cary Businesses Fear Displacement by Rail Crossing Project

Longtime businesses near Maynard Road and Chatham Street in Cary say an NCDOT railroad crossing proposal could push them out. ABC11 reports the $84 million plan would lower Maynard Road, build a bridge and create a new intersection, with construction possibly starting in 2030. Affected shops include furniture stores, barbershops and Mexican, Indian and Nepali restaurants such as The Sea Depot and Esmeralda Grill. The crossing was flagged for upgrades after safety incidents, including a May collision between a train and a construction trailer. Owners have submitted comments and raised concerns with the Cary Town Council, and NCDOT says community feedback is part of its project development process.

🎓 NC State Suspends Sigma Nu Chapter Amid Hazing Probe

NC State has suspended the Sigma Nu Beta Tau fraternity chapter after finding it responsible for violating parts of the student code of conduct tied to hazing allegations. ABC11 reports that university police are conducting a separate criminal investigation, and the national organization has also suspended the chapter's activities. CBS17 likewise reports the chapter is under investigation. University police did not answer questions about arrests or a timeline, and students said the allegations are a major campus topic.

🏛 Early Voting in North Carolina Outpaces 2022

With one week until the midterms, North Carolina early voting is running well ahead of 2022. ABC11 reports more than 1.77 million ballots had been cast as of Monday, about 37 percent above the same point four years ago. Wake County leads the state with over 222,000 ballots across a record 18 early voting sites for a midterm. Democrats account for about 41 percent of ballots, unaffiliated voters just under 35 percent and Republicans nearly 25 percent. Early voting runs through 3 p.m. Saturday, Oct. 31.

Tuesday, October 27, 2026

🏗 Red Hat Amphitheater Closes as Convention Center Expansion Advances

The Red Hat Amphitheater on Cabarrus Street has permanently closed, clearing the way for the expanded Raleigh Convention Center. The city says contractors will begin mobilizing for demolition in about two weeks, with a groundbreaking planned for next month. The replacement amphitheater is being built one block south and is expected to open in June, while the expansion itself is scheduled to open at the start of 2030. An Omni Hotel already under construction is due in late 2028, and the convention sales team is fielding interest for events in 2029 through 2031. Lodging, food and beverage tax collections are running 4% ahead of Wake County projections this fiscal year.

🏈 NC State Turns to Interim Coach After Doeren Firing

N.C. State is looking ahead under interim head coach Todd Goebbel after firing Dave Doeren midseason. Goebbel has been on the staff since 2019, most recently as running backs coach and special teams coordinator. The Wolfpack are 3-4 overall and 1-3 in the ACC. Their next game is Saturday at home against California.

🏒 Hurricanes Return Home After Shutout in St. Louis

The Carolina Hurricanes come home to Lenovo Center tonight to face Boston, their first home game in two weeks, after blanking the Blues in St. Louis behind Pyotr Kochetkov on Sunday. The club also activated Taylor Hall on Monday, and the injury report says he is hopeful to return Tuesday. Carolina signed Nikishin to a one-year contract over the weekend and assigned Skyler Brind'Amour to the Wolves.

🚨 Raleigh Home Fire Under Investigation After Reported Boom

Firefighters battled a second-floor house fire on Coleraine Court in Raleigh Sunday afternoon, with flames visible shortly after 4 p.m. Neighbors reported a loud boom and popping sounds beforehand, though officials have not confirmed an explosion. The homeowner said the house is rented to a family he believed was out of town, and one neighbor saw a man with a dog leave safely. Authorities have not confirmed whether anyone was inside, and the cause remains under investigation.

🤝 State Fair Wraps Up 11-Day Run in Raleigh

The North Carolina State Fair ended its 11-day run Sunday with thousands on the midway for the final day. Attendance through the first 10 days was just under 818,000, and fair director Kent Yelverton said nearly a million people walk past vendors over the run. Some vendors did not return this year, and it is unclear whether a new 15% license fee on gross food and beverage sales played a role. Final attendance figures were not yet available.

🤝 Durham Gets Its First FRINGE Festival This Week

Durham's inaugural FRINGE Festival runs Oct. 30 through Nov. 8, with about 50 artists performing in small downtown venues. Organized by the Carolina Theatre, the lineup spans shadow puppetry, poetry theatre, experimental dance, live music and a drag opera, and artists are paid a fee to perform. Some events are free and others ticketed, with no single festival pass. Cary also held its first Diwali celebration at Downtown Cary Park, drawing hundreds.
