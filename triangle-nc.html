🚨 NCCU Active Shooter Alert Deemed Unfounded

North Carolina Central University sent students a “run, hide, fight” active shooter alert at 3:15 p.m. Sunday, and Durham police later called the report “unfounded,” WRAL reports. About 10 minutes after the first alert, a second one said no shots had been fired on campus and told people to shelter in place while officers searched for an armed suspect who had reportedly fled toward the university. NCCU police said the lockdown was a precaution after Durham police responded to reported gunfire on Lincoln Street near campus. A sweep found no one on campus or nearby, no one was hurt, and police said there would be extra officers on campus for now.

🚨 Two Serious Weekend Crashes on Raleigh Roads

A pedestrian was killed early Sunday at S. Saunders Street and Carolina Pines Avenue in Raleigh, and police are still looking for the driver who fled, according to WRAL. Authorities have not released the victim’s name. In a separate crash around 3 a.m. Sunday, an impaired driver hit another vehicle head-on at Western Boulevard and the I-440 Beltline and injured three people, arrest warrants say. The 21-year-old driver from Candler had a blood alcohol content of 0.13. He faces a DWI charge and three counts of felony serious injury by vehicle and is being held on a $160,000 bond.

🏒 Aho Becomes Canes’ Top Goal-Scorer in Winning Weekend

Sebastian Aho scored the 323rd goal of his career on Saturday against Edmonton, passing Eric Staal for the most goals by a Hurricanes player since the franchise moved to North Carolina, Hurricanes.com reports. It was a power-play game-winner in a three-point night, and Ron Francis still holds the franchise record with 382. On Sunday Carolina won 5-2 over the Rangers at Madison Square Garden. Eric Robinson, Jackson Blake, Alexander Nikishin, Sean Walker and Jordan Martinook scored, and Brandon Bussi made 25 saves, per the team’s recap. The Canes became the first NHL team this season to reach 100 goals, and Aho has 33 points through 24 games, his best start in 11 seasons.

🏀 UNC Routs Monmouth as Duke Slides in Poll

North Carolina beat Monmouth 85-45 Monday night, led by Terrance Brown’s 20 points and a 10-point, 11-rebound double-double from Alex Samodurov, WRAL reports. The Tar Heels led 51-15 at halftime and are now 5-1. They play West Virginia on Friday at the Dick Vitale Invitational in Charlotte. In Durham, Duke fell 11 spots to No. 16 in Monday’s AP Top 25, the biggest drop of the week, after Illinois ended its 35-game home winning streak and the Blue Devils had a closer-than-expected win over Washington State.

🤝 Thanksgiving Travelers at RDU Uneasy About Delays

Travelers heading out of Raleigh-Durham International Airport for one of the busiest weeks of the year say they are nervous after recent flight disruptions, WRAL reports. The worry follows a week of FAA-related problems that caused thousands of delays and cancellations across the country. Most recently, an automation problem at Delta led to more than 1,100 delayed flights at Atlanta’s Hartsfield-Jackson airport. Officials suggest checking flight status often and leaving extra time at the airport this week.

🤝 Raleigh Police Retire Their Last Crown Victoria

The Raleigh Police Department has taken its last Ford Crown Victoria off patrol, WUNC reports. The car is a 2011 Police Interceptor, one of the last the city bought before Ford stopped making the model that year. The department will keep it for community events instead of selling it at auction. Officer Jeffrey “J.C.” Vucich, who started his career in a Crown Victoria and was one of the last officers to drive one, asked to keep it, and Chief Rico Boyce backed the idea. The fleet now relies mostly on Ford Explorer-based interceptors.
