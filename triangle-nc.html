🏒 Canes Rally Past Lightning for Historic Pre-Thanksgiving Mark

The Hurricanes scored three unanswered goals in Tampa on Wednesday night to beat the Lightning 3-1 and improve to 19-3-3. After Anthony Cirelli gave Tampa Bay a power-play lead early in the second period, Eric Robinson tied it at 5:24 of the third, Jaccob Slavin scored his first goal of the season less than two minutes later, and Jackson Blake added an empty-netter. Brandon Bussi made a season-high 32 saves for his fourth straight win. Only the 2001-02 Red Wings and 1983-84 Oilers had reached 19 wins before Thanksgiving, and the result comes days after Sebastian Aho became the franchise's all-time leading goal-scorer.

🌡 Rain and Delays Greet Thanksgiving Travelers at RDU

Thanksgiving Eve crowds packed Raleigh-Durham International Airport on Wednesday as severe weather on both coasts caused delays and cancellations nationwide. AAA estimated nearly 6 million people would fly over the holiday period, and some travelers paid hundreds of dollars to switch to earlier flights rather than risk getting stranded. On the ground, soaking rain and gusty winds made for messy driving on I-40, with AAA projecting about 72 million people on the road. The State Highway Patrol has launched a holiday seat belt enforcement campaign and warned drivers about impaired, speeding and drowsy driving.

🚨 Garner Man Charged With Threatening Wake DA

Raleigh police arrested 36-year-old Demetrious Hannan of Garner and charged him with threatening to kill Wake County District Attorney Lorrin Freeman in a Facebook video. Police said he told Freeman "I'm going to punish you for everyone else's kids," and that officers believed he was likely to carry out the threat after earlier remarks about guns and killing her. Hannan was already out on bond for a trespassing charge at a county office inside the Wake County Justice Center. He is being held on a $150,000 bond and is due in court December 14; Freeman declined to comment, and Wiley Nickel will succeed her as DA in 2027.

🚨 Futura Lane House Fire Displaces Six in Raleigh

A Wednesday morning fire on Futura Lane in Raleigh displaced four adults and two children and sent one firefighter to the hospital with minor injuries. The fire was reported shortly after 9:30 a.m., and crews brought it under control within 40 minutes. Officials said it may have started on the back side of the home, and the Raleigh Fire Department is still investigating the cause. The Red Cross is assisting the family.

🏛 Wake at Home Houses Encampment Residents Ahead of Freezing Nights

After closing a second encampment, this one near a busy road in South Raleigh, Wake County's Wake at Home initiative says 29 people from the site are in or will soon be in safe housing, following six to eight weeks of daily outreach by the city's Care Navigation Team. The effort has $22 million in established funding plus a $12 million housing bond Raleigh voters approved this month. Wake County activated its White Flag emergency shelters for the second time this season through Thursday, and Healing Transitions is adding 50 drop-in beds at each of its men's and women's shelters on those nights. The initiative's first phase aims to move 400 people from encampments into stable housing by December 2027.

🤝 Durham Rescue Mission Serves Thanksgiving Feast to Thousands

The Durham Rescue Mission is holding its 52nd annual Thanksgiving Feast today, with organizers expecting to serve more than 2,000 people in tents in the mission's parking lot. Doors open at 11 a.m. and the meal is served at noon, and more than 600 volunteers are grilling 150 turkeys and handing out groceries and free clothing. Organizers say the event goes on rain or shine and will move indoors if there is a downpour. They and local pantry workers say rising grocery and gas costs, along with cuts to SNAP and Medicaid benefits, are pushing more families to seek help.
