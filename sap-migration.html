🔧 Security

onapsis

Onapsis sells SAP application security and compliance for S/4HANA, RISE and BTP. Its Research Labs regularly disclose SAP vulnerabilities.

Press releases (last 10 days): No company press release; the newest on the press page is from Oct 8. There is significant research news in the window, though. On Nov 10, SAP's Patch Day fixed four CVSS 10.0 flaws (The Hacker News, BleepingComputer). Onapsis found three of them:

  • ICM2SHELL (CVE-2026-76964 and CVE-2026-89126), a WebSocket reassembly bug in ICM and Web Dispatcher. Onapsis gave SAP a working exploit that runs OS commands as <sid>adm.
  • PROXYGATE (CVE-2026-89103) in SAP Gateway.

New product developments: March 17, 2026: Onapsis Agentic Gateway. June 2026: Q2 release, which adds Web Dispatcher monitoring in Defend and one-click compensating controls for misconfigurations. Correction to last week: the Secure RISE Accelerator dates from Nov 2024.

How it uses AI: The Agentic Gateway is an MCP server. Through it, approved assistants (Copilot, Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or custom agents) can query Onapsis threat intelligence and a customer's SAP security data in natural language. They can cross-reference new zero-days against the landscape and draft remediation plans. The release does not say agents can apply fixes. Security Advisor's "AI-driven" trend analysis is not explained.
