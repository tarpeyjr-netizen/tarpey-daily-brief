How it uses AI: Zscaler's AI is applied to inline traffic inspection across its cloud — classifying and scoring sessions, detecting anomalous access patterns and applying data-loss-prevention classification on content in transit, trained on the aggregate traffic volume crossing the Zero Trust Exchange. None of this is SAP-specific; the AI operates at the network and access layer regardless of the application behind it.
