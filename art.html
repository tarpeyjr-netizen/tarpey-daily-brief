Cow (Vaca)

ArtistÉmile Claus (1849–1924)
StyleLuminism / Belgian Impressionism
YearUndated
MuseumSão Paulo Museum of Art (MASP), São Paulo, Brazil
Interesting Fact

Émile Claus was the leading figure of Belgian Luminism, a sun-drenched offshoot of French Impressionism, and he painted many of his pastoral scenes from his home at Astene on the River Leie. When the First World War broke out he went into exile in London, where he continued to paint, including views of the Thames. This oil of a lone cow is a quiet example of the rural subjects he returned to throughout his career, and it carries MASP accession number MASP.00195.
