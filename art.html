The Rescue of William D'Oyly

ArtistJohn Wilson Carmichael (c. 1799–1868)
StyleBritish maritime and historical painting
Year1841
MuseumNational Gallery of Australia, Canberra, Australia
Interesting Fact

D'Oyly was just three years old when the barque Charles Eaton struck the Great Barrier Reef in August 1834; most of those aboard were killed by Torres Strait Islanders, and he survived with only one other boy, John Ireland. The two lived on Murray Island (Mer) for about two years until Captain Charles Lewis of the colonial schooner Isabella recovered them in June 1836. Carmichael, better known for naval scenes, painted the rescue in 1841, and the National Gallery of Australia bought it in 2010.
