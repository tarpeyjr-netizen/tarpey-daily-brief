Along the River During the Qingming Festival (Qing Court Version), detail

ArtistChen Mei (1694–1745), Sun Hu, Jin Kun, Dai Hong and Cheng Zhidao, painters of the Qing court Painting Academy
StyleQing court painting; handscroll, ink and colors on silk
Year1736 (presented to the Qianlong Emperor in January 1737)
MuseumNational Palace Museum, Taipei, Taiwan
Interesting Fact

Commissioned by the Yongzheng Emperor, this 11-meter scroll was finished only after his death and presented to his son, the Qianlong Emperor, on January 15, 1737. The five court painters worked from an ink draft by fellow court artist Shen Yuan and packed in more than 4,000 people, far more than the Song-dynasty original, along with Western-style buildings and architecture drawn according to the rules of perspective. Its leftmost third leaves the bustling city behind for an imperial palace that appears nowhere in the earlier version.
