Odalisque

ArtistJules Joseph Lefebvre (1836–1911)
StyleAcademicism / Orientalism
Year1874
MuseumArt Institute of Chicago, Chicago, USA
Interesting Fact

This languid reclining nude once hung in the New York mansion of Vincent Astor before it was sold at auction in 1926 to Chicago collector George F. Harding Jr., who displayed it in his eccentric private museum built mostly around suits of medieval armor. It sat there, strangely out of place among the armor and weaponry, until the Harding collection was folded into the Art Institute of Chicago in 1983. Lefebvre borrowed the very same reclining pose Ingres had used decades earlier for his scandalous Grande Odalisque, and while this version caused no comparable uproar, it helped cement Lefebvre's reputation as one of Paris's most sought-after teachers, with more than 1,500 students passing through his studio, many of them American.
