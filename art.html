Imaginary View of the Grande Galerie of the Louvre in Ruins

ArtistHubert Robert (1733–1808)
StyleCapriccio — architectural ruin fantasy
Year1796
MuseumMusée du Louvre, Paris, France
Interesting Fact

Robert was the Louvre's own curator when he painted his workplace as a shattered ruin open to the sky — and he exhibited it beside a companion canvas showing the same gallery gloriously renovated, a before-and-after argument for his museum design. Nicknamed "Robert des Ruines" for his obsession with decay, he had just survived imprisonment during the Terror; by one account he escaped the guillotine only because another prisoner was executed in his place by mistake. Amid the rubble, a lone artist calmly sketches the Apollo Belvedere — Robert's hint that art outlives even the museums built to hold it.
