South Wind (Nanpū)

ArtistWada Sanzō (1883–1967)
StyleYōga (Western-style Japanese painting), Academic Realism
Year1907
MuseumThe National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT) — Tokyo, Japan
Interesting Fact

Wada was 24 when this canvas took the top prize at the very first Bunten, Japan's inaugural government-sponsored art exhibition — an overnight arrival that made him a national name. The shipwrecked sailors were drawn from experience: in 1902 the boat carrying Wada toward Hachijōjima was caught in a storm and drifted for three days before washing ashore, though sources still disagree over whether he landed on Ōshima or Hachijōjima. Decades later Wada abandoned the heroic nude for color science, compiling the six-volume Dictionary of Color Combinations whose palettes are still mined by designers today.
