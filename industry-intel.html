🏢 Consulting & Defense Intel

Capgemini

On Nov 17 Capgemini agreed to acquire 100% of Singapore-based Cloud4C, a hybrid-cloud platform services provider. No price was disclosed, and India's Competition Commission still has to approve it (ad-hoc-news). The same day it released its TechnoVision 2027 trends report. An unconfirmed single-source report claims about 100 redundancies in Australia (The Aussie Corporate). Treat that as rumor until it is confirmed.

Accenture

Accenture plans to cut about 950 jobs in Portugal by January through voluntary exits for staff serving Meta. About 1,400 Lisbon Trust & Safety employees are eligible (Reuters via The Star). Accenture Federal Services hired FAA veteran Terry Biggio as managing director and lead for transportation and aviation (ExecutiveBiz). There was no new SAP acquisition this week.

Deloitte

No material news this week.

IBM Consulting

IBM released a study of chief AI officers on Nov 17. It found that CAIOs can see where AI creates value but few control the investment decisions (IBM Newsroom). It is useful framing for AI-on-SAP business cases. There were no deals, leadership moves or A&D/Life Sciences wins in the window.

Infosys

On Nov 17 Infosys said Gartner had named it a Leader in the 2026 Magic Quadrant for Public Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services, for the fourth year in a row (Infosys). Management attended investor forums in Mumbai and Singapore on Nov 16–19 (Investywise). The CEO handover to Ashiss Kumar Dash is still pending.

Boeing & ULA

  • Vulcan launch failure. ULA's return-to-flight launch on Nov 15 lost all 40 Amazon Leo satellites. It is ULA's first total mission failure. ULA cited low core-stage performance and on Nov 18 pointed to inadequate fuel supply to the BE-4 engines. The FAA oversees the investigation (GeekWire, Spaceflight Now). SAP angle: a failure investigation leans on serialized as-built records, nonconformance and genealogy data. Expect pressure on quality and traceability in ULA's systems, and a slower manifest that delays revenue.
  • Philippine Airlines finalizes up to 20 787s on Nov 15. The White House valued the deal at about $12B (Boeing). SAP angle: more 787 backlog adds to widebody production planning and supplier MRP load.
  • More layoffs possible; 737 rate goal slips. Boeing would not tell SPEEA whether more cuts will follow the Dec 9 layoff notices. The 5% Commercial Airplanes reduction is tied to the 737 rate increase from 42 to 47 a month slipping to early 2027 (Reuters via KFGO). SAP angle: cost pressure in Commercial Airplanes usually defers discretionary IT and ERP spend.

DFARS & interchangeability

  • DFARS: quiet. The Federal Register shows no DARS documents published Nov 1–22 (Federal Register). DARS's open-cases list (as of Nov 6) shows an unnamed draft final rule sent to OIRA on Oct 26, which could publish soon (DARS open cases). The CMMC Phase 2 suspension (Class Deviation 2026-O0025 Rev 3, Sept) still stands.
  • Interchangeability: no rule or standard changes in the window.

Defense budget

  • The continuing resolution expires Dec 11. The CR (P.L. 119-103) funds DoD at FY26 levels and bars new production starts and rate increases. On Nov 17 Akin Gump framed the lame-duck choice as finishing FY27 bills or passing another CR that leaves decisions to the incoming majority (Akin Gump). SAP angle: another CR would keep new-start sustainment and modernization work, and its funding lines, on hold into 2027.
  • FY27 NDAA: the conferenced text is expected in "a couple weeks" alongside funding action, according to a Sen. Kaine staffer (ExchangeMonitor). AFA is pressing for multiyear procurement authority (AFA).

Hiring signals

  • SAP's chief customer officer to exit. Thomas Saueressig, who runs Sales, Customer Success and Customer Services & Delivery, will leave the Executive Board; responsibilities start moving in January and he stays until end of March 2027. Emmanuel Raptopoulos takes over on an interim basis. A change at the top of SAP Services and Customer Success can reshuffle senior delivery and RISE adoption leadership in 2027. SAP News (Nov 17)
  • Takeda moves from ECC to SAP Cloud ERP under RISE. SAP runs nearly all of Takeda's manufacturing, supply chain, finance and quality processes worldwide. No SI is named yet. A large regulated-pharma RISE program is the kind of work that drives demand for Life Sciences program directors. SAP News (Nov 20)
  • Capgemini signs for full ownership of Cloud4C. The agreement was signed Nov 17 and needs Indian competition approval. It adds hybrid-cloud hosting capacity behind RISE and managed-service deals. ad-hoc-news (Nov 18)
  • SAP closes its TechWolf acquisition. TechWolf is a skills-intelligence AI vendor. The deal points to more AI-driven skills matching inside SuccessFactors, which matters for HCM-heavy S/4HANA programs. SAP News (Nov 16)
  • Accenture cuts ~950 Meta-linked jobs in Portugal. The cut runs through a voluntary-exit scheme by January and is tied to lower client demand for Trust & Safety work. It is not SAP-related, but it shows Accenture trimming low-margin operations work while it keeps buying SAP capacity (e.g., McCoy). Reuters via The Star (Nov 19)

💡 Independent Work

Platforms

  • Upwork: no fee or program changes this week. It announced its CFO and Chief Business Officer will speak at UBS on Dec 2 (GlobeNewswire, Nov 19). Upwork Now, launched Nov 2, is still its newest expert-hiring product.
  • No changes: Toptal, Catalant, Business Talent Group, freelancermap, GLG, AlphaSights, Guidepoint, Third Bridge, Coleman Research.

Demand

  • The Fractional Work Report 2026 puts fractional hiring demand up 149% year over year in Q1 2026. 97% of postings come from small and mid-sized companies, and finding clients is still the main constraint (Lightnow, Fractional Jobs). These are vendor sources, and no new data came out this week.
  • Only 24% of Fractional Jobs clients ask explicitly for AI skills, but 38% of fractional workers say AI expertise helped them win work (HumanCloud). Pairing AI with deep domain work, such as AI on SAP, is the more defensible pitch.
  • Large RISE moves in regulated industries, like Takeda this week, create demand for independent QA, validation and program-assurance roles alongside the main SI (SAP News).

Claude-built tools making money
