Boeing & ULA

  • Vulcan failure leaves SpaceX as the only certified option for critical national-security launches. ULA CEO Mark Peller described "a significant shortfall in booster, or first-stage performance" and said the solid boosters performed as expected. New Glenn has been grounded since a May pad explosion. That leaves Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy as the only rockets cleared for critical NSSL Phase 3 Lane 2 payloads, and analysts expect Vulcan to stay grounded for months (Breaking Defense, Nov 16–17). SAP angle: a long stand-down shifts ULA's work toward failure analysis, rework and inventory carrying costs. Expect pressure on project-systems cost collection, nonconformance tracking and revenue-recognition timing for missions that slip.

⚙️ Claude & Automation Playbook

Computer use for Team and Enterprise (beta)

Claude can now see your screen and work in desktop apps you approve one by one, such as Excel, PowerPoint or a browser, on Team and Enterprise plans. It runs from the Claude desktop app on Mac and Windows. A workspace owner turns it on under Organization settings → Capabilities, and it is off by default. There is no sandbox between Claude and your apps, so only approve apps you would let an assistant use.

Why it matters to you: much SAP program admin happens in tools with no connector, such as status decks, RAID spreadsheets and timesheet portals, and Claude can now work in those directly.

Try this: once enabled, have Claude open the weekly RAID workbook in Excel and the status deck in PowerPoint, and update the deck's red/amber/green table and top-five risks from the workbook. Approve only those two apps and keep it off client systems (an hour) · Source

Release channel in Claude Desktop

You can now choose how fast the desktop app updates. "Stable," the default, gets tested versions less often. "Latest" updates about once a day, so new features reach you sooner.

Why it matters to you: new Cowork and computer-use features often roll out gradually, so "Latest" on a personal machine gets them to you first, while a work laptop can stay on the steadier build.

Try this: switch your personal Mac to "Latest" and leave the machine that runs the Daily Brief routines on "Stable," so an untested build can't break a scheduled run (10 min) · Source

Pattern of the week: meeting → whiteboard → tracker

The workflow in launch coverage of Claude Meetings, Whiteboards and Sheets (Nov 10–12) is a chain. Claude sits in the call as a visible participant, fills a shared whiteboard with decisions and open items as people talk, and then turns the board into a document, a deck or a tracker with tabs and formulas. That makes the follow-up the product, not the minutes (TechCrunch, Claude blog). Meetings requires a Team or Enterprise plan and works in Google Meet. Coverage says materials Claude creates during calls are free through Feb 10, 2027 (citybiz). No independent hands-on reports or time-saved figures exist yet. First step: in your next fit-gap workshop on Google Meet, have Claude log decisions and open items on a whiteboard, then convert it into a RAID tracker in Claude Sheets before the call ends.
