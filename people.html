Week ending Wednesday August 26, 2026

This Week

Tyler Cowen had the week's most unusual podcast run — two long sessions with Rick Rubin recorded in Tuscany, including one in which Cowen DJs an entire episode on music and patriotism, plus a 52-minute turn on the Two Percent Podcast about "wellness." Updates

Jerry Saltz — Artist

Update: Fantastic Man published a long feature on Saltz on August 25, crowning him "the internet's favourite art critic." The piece argues he became New York magazine's star art writer precisely by dispensing with intellectual pretension — trademark candour, humour and self-deprecation — and notes he still sees over a thousand exhibitions a year, splitting his days between looking at art and writing about it. He has held the senior art critic post since 2006.

Michael Lewis — Author

Update: Promotion is ramping for "Blockers: Rebels in the Deep State," out October 6 from Norton and Pushkin. He also sat with Nicolle Wallace at Symphony Space for her podcast "The Best People," revisiting the characters of "The Big Short" for the new season of his own show, Against the Rules.
