Week of Oct 31 to Nov 07, 2026

Politics

  • The Dáil voted 79 to 71 on 21 October for a bill to end the “triple lock” on Irish troops serving abroad; it now goes to committee, with government aiming for law by year’s end (Rio Times explainer).
  • The chief whip has published the priority legislation list to Christmas, including e-scooters and a citizenship-tightening bill.
  • Ireland holds the EU Council presidency until 31 December under the slogan “Strength with unity”.
  • Note: little politics reporting dated this week surfaced in searches; items above are the latest found.

Economics

  • Unemployment for October was 4.8%, up from a revised 4.7% in September, per Irish Examiner coverage of the CSO estimates.
  • Harmonised inflation was estimated at 3.9% in the 12 months to September, well above the 2% target (Chartered Accountants Ireland).
  • The State fiscal watchdog warned that Budget 2027 measures put public finances on a worse trajectory.

Military / Defence Forces

  • About 350 personnel were due to deploy to UNIFIL in Lebanon in November; the UN mission’s mandate ends 31 December 2026 (Irish Examiner).
  • Minister Helen McEntee has admitted the end of the mission will likely prompt an exodus, with about 450 personnel expected to leave next year (report).
  • A radar acquisition order was signed with France; deliveries begin in 2027 with full capability by end-2028, and four Airbus H145M helicopters are planned for 2027–28.
  • Budget 2027 gave Defence a record €1.6bn.

Technology

  • Commentary argues Ireland is unusually exposed to restructuring at Meta and TikTok, as three multinationals paid 46% of 2024 corporation tax (AI Weekly).
  • IDA announcements continue to roll in, including a Dublin HQ for Trade EXL with up to 200 AI and data roles (TechCentral).

Week of Sat 24 to Sat 31 Oct, 2026

Politics

  • Budget 2027 was presented to the Dáil on 6 October with Simon Harris as Minister for Finance; reports put the package at about €8.5 billion (€7 billion extra spending, €1.5 billion tax measures).
  • A Rio Times explainer says the Dáil backed a bill 79 to 71 on 21 October; the bill was not named in the coverage found, so details are unconfirmed.
  • The Government is a Fianna Fáil, Fine Gael and Regional Independent Group coalition. Little else specific to this week surfaced in searches.

Economics

  • CSO figures show consumer inflation hit 4.1% in September, the highest in over 30 months, with housing, water and energy up 9.5%.
  • The Central Bank forecasts inflation easing to 3.1% in 2027 and 2% in 2028, while the Department of Finance expects about 2%. Economists warn inflation may "wash away" budget measures.
  • The Fiscal Council warned inflation and a new public sector pay deal could add up to €8 billion of cost pressure in 2027.

Military / Defence Forces

  • A September digest reported Defence Forces budget pressures, including reduced rations and a paused naval radar plan (citing the Irish Times; not independently confirmed).
  • About 350 Irish troops serve with UNIFIL in Lebanon; the UN mandate was renewed only to the end of 2026, after which the mission is to wind down.
