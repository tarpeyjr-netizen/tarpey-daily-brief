🔥 Trending in Ireland
Saoirse Ní Fhaoláin
Music · Galway

Saoirse Ní Fhaoláin is a Galway sean-nós vocalist fusing unaccompanied traditional Irish singing with ambient electronic production — a combination that shouldn't work on paper but has been drawing real word-of-mouth buzz across the west of Ireland's independent music scene. Her debut EP, Cósta, released in February 2026, earned widespread critical acclaim for treading a line between reverence for the sean-nós tradition and a genuinely contemporary sound. Momentum built organically through Galway's gig circuit rather than a label push, culminating in a sold-out show at the city's Róisín Dubh in March. She's now cited among the Irish artists to watch for 2026, a rare case of traditional Irish-language singing finding a young, electronic-leaning audience.

🔥 Trending in Ireland
Dove Ellis
Music · Ireland

Dove Ellis is the Irish singer-songwriter behind the debut album Blizzard, a dreamy, '70s-influenced indie record that's built momentum entirely from the ground up. Rather than a label push, her rise has come from an organic cult following and constant word-of-mouth chatter online, the kind of grassroots buzz that tastemaker outlets are now flagging as a breakout signal for 2026. It fits a wider shift in Irish music this year: fans burned out on polished, algorithm-driven pop are gravitating toward artists who feel raw and unfiltered, and Ellis's slow-building, listener-led spread is being cited as a blueprint for how an Irish indie act can go from niche to festival-ready without a major marketing budget.
