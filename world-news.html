Friday, October 23, 2026 · South America

Colombia

South America · population ~52M

Politics & Government

President Abelardo de la Espriella, a conservative sworn in on August 7 under a heavy security cordon, is now in his third month, having won a tight runoff against Iván Cepeda. He has moved to close the peace-negotiation tables his predecessor opened with illegal armed groups, arguing the peace policy failed. Analysts quoted in coverage of the election result expect the narrow margin and a Congress where the Pacto Histórico holds the most seats to make major reforms hard to pass without a coalition.

Economy & Business

Inflation remains the central problem: Forbes Colombia reports September annual inflation of 6.29%, with the central bank warning it could stay above its 3% target until 2028 and that the peso may weaken. One report says the Banco de la República raised its policy rate to 12.25% from 12.00% on September 30, and the peso has been trading near 3,300 per dollar after a strong rally this year. Those figures come from secondary outlets and are worth checking against banrep.gov.co.

Military & Security

On October 19 the president ordered a “total offensive” against the ELN and FARC dissidents, branding them narco-terrorists. He ruled out any prisoner swap and demanded the release of officials the ELN is holding, and he ordered Arauca guerrilla leaders moved to maximum-security prisons.

Society & Culture

Unions, teachers and students held a national day of protest in mid-October. According to Diario del Sur, demands include rejecting possible labour and pension changes, better health care for teachers (Fecode), funding for public universities and the 2027 minimum-wage talks. I found no closing report with turnout figures.

Tourism

Regional demand is growing: Mexico, Brazil and Argentina sent more than 545,000 visitors from January to August, up 7.1%, 18% and 3.6% respectively. Colombia is also in the running for several awards, with public voting at the World Travel Awards open until October 30.

Site to visit: Port, Fortresses and Group of Monuments, Cartagena is a UNESCO World Heritage Site on the Caribbean coast, combining a walled colonial city with the huge San Felipe de Barajas fortress. The old town is walkable and best explored early morning or evening to avoid the heat; flights from Bogotá take about an hour and a half.

Cartagena indie music scene (Hamilton)
Music · Cartagena

Cartagena musician Hamilton was among the independent artists YouTube picked for its 2026 Foundry programme, which supports emerging independents. Cartagena's Afrobeat singer Ian Escobar has also drawn attention beyond Colombia with his single “Moscow” and an early-2026 debut EP. I could not confirm a specific October breakout, so treat this as a scene to watch rather than a verified viral moment.

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · North America & Asia

Cuba

North America · population ~12M

Politics & Government

Havana is pushing back hard ahead of the annual UN General Assembly vote on the US embargo, expected on October 27 or 28. Foreign Minister Bruno Rodríguez accused Washington of pressuring member states with threats such as visa denials and the suspension of cooperation programmes, allegations the State Department had not answered at publication. US ambassador Mike Waltz meanwhile said Cuba “propagates a lie” through the resolution and acknowledged lobbying European countries to reconsider their support. Separately, Florida's attorney general announced a state arrest warrant for former leader Raúl Castro over the 1996 Brothers to the Rescue shootdown, a case that sits alongside an existing federal indictment; the 95-year-old Castro is not expected to appear in a Florida court.

Economy & Business

US pressure on Cuba's finances tightened again, with Washington sanctioning the state-run bank BICSA over alleged Iranian links, a move Havana's foreign minister called collective punishment. CBS News reported from Havana that bread prices have risen tenfold this year while a typical state salary is under $20 a month, and that roughly two-thirds of Cubans depend on state employers. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has said the campaign is designed to leave the government with “no escape valves,” and talks between the two sides are reportedly at a standstill. Cuba has opened some room for the private sector, but its leaders say political reform is off the table.

Society & Culture

Daily life is dominated by blackouts and shortages, with most areas including Havana receiving only a few hours of electricity per day. Doctors at a children's hospital told CBS News they can no longer obtain some lifesaving paediatric drugs, and some patients turn to community pharmacies or the black market, as set out in the network's report. A House of Commons Library briefing notes that outages of up to 20 hours have forced hospitals to postpone non-emergency surgery and that protests in 2026 have been limited, while Human Rights Watch says hundreds of critics remain arbitrarily detained.

Tourism

Cuba's tourism sector is in deep trouble. Official ONEI figures show 450,353 international visitors from January to August, down 64.4%, with arrivals of just 30,490 in August and Canada, the biggest market, down 75.6%. Fuel shortages led several airlines to suspend flights earlier this year, and the prime minister said in July that 73% of hotel facilities were closed. ECLAC projects a 10.3% contraction of the economy in 2026, so anyone planning a trip should check flight availability, power and fuel conditions and official travel advisories first.

Site to visit: Old Havana (La Habana Vieja) is the colonial core of the capital and a UNESCO World Heritage Site with its fortifications, home to the plazas, baroque churches and Spanish forts that trace the city's history from the sixteenth century. It is best explored on foot, starting from the Plaza de la Catedral and working toward the Malecón seafront. Expect long daily power cuts and thin services at present, so carry cash and plan around the blackouts.

🔥 Trending
Reparto dance trend
Music & social media · Havana's neighbourhoods

Reparto, a Cuban urban music style that emerged in Havana's working-class neighbourhoods between roughly 2007 and 2015, blends reggaeton with timba, rumba and guaguancó and has spread largely through mobile internet and social media. The latest wave is driven by tracks such as “Dame un AAA (Tita)” by Bebeshito and Ya Ice Dilan, which has nearly 750,000 YouTube views since its March release and is now inspiring dance clips from outside the island, including a Barcelona creator filming herself dancing it for her Cuban fiancé. The momentum is organic, spread by fans on TikTok and Instagram rather than by any official promotion.

Uzbekistan

Asia · population ~36M

Politics & Government

Diplomacy has been busy. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited Samarkand on October 19 for talks with Central Asian foreign ministers in the C5+1 format, and President Mirziyoyev later met UN General Assembly President Khalilur Rahman. At the SCO heads of government meeting in Dushanbe, Prime Minister Abdulla Aripov proposed an SCO development strategy to 2040, and the organisation's chairmanship is set to pass to Uzbekistan, which will host the 2027 meeting. At home, Mirziyoyev also called for wider use of Uzbek in science and digital technologies.

Economy & Business

The headline was the Rubio visit, where Uzbek officials said 54 agreements worth about $12 billion were signed across energy, critical minerals, aviation, finance and digital technology. Reports note that the figure is a stated value rather than money already invested, and some deals still need financing. Domestic data was upbeat as well: electricity generation rose 8.4% to 69.6 billion kWh in nine months, and Russia remains the leading export market at $3.2 billion. A US company also plans a data center in the Samarkand region.

Military & Security

Security cooperation featured mainly in a regional setting. Uzbekistan's SCO proposals in Dushanbe included a coordination mechanism for food, water and energy security and backing for moving the SCO Development Bank toward launch. At home, authorities said they plan to expand rescue services to more reservoirs.

Society & Culture

New statistics show that marriages fell to 267,100 in 2025, the lowest in seven years and down 44,300 from the 2018 peak of 311,400, with 92,300 registered in the first half of 2026. In parliament, MPs raised concerns over landfill safety and waste collection fees. Uzbekistan and UNICEF also discussed improving services for children with disabilities.

Tourism

Visitor numbers keep climbing. Euronews, citing the Tourism Committee, reports 9.1 million visits by foreign nationals in the first eight months of 2026, up 21.1%, with tourism services exports up 53.5% to $4.6 billion. Most arrivals are from neighbouring countries, but China, Malaysia and Japan are the fastest-growing markets, and visitors are venturing beyond Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva. A high-speed train to Khiva that began running in May cut the trip from Tashkent to about 7.5 hours.

Site to visit: Itchan Kala, the walled inner city of Khiva, covers about 26 hectares of mosques, madrasas and minarets and was the first place in Central Asia to become a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Highlights include the climbable Islam Khodja Minaret and the Juma Mosque with its 213 carved wooden pillars. March and November are the best months, and the new high-speed train station is about a 20-minute walk from the old town.

Science & Technology

At the Samarkand business council, Rubio proposed introducing American small modular nuclear reactors alongside grid and gas network modernisation to meet fast-rising energy demand. Connectivity is also advancing, as mobile internet now covers 99.5% of populated areas, and Uzbekistan and South Korea discussed digitalising transport infrastructure.

🔥 Trending
Makon AI
EdTech startup · Gulistan / Tashkent

Makon AI, an AI platform for SAT and ACT preparation, grew out of a Telegram-based tutoring business started in summer 2024 by Asilbek Ashurov, who grew up in the small city of Gulistan. According to a founder interview, the product launched around late 2025, now counts over 50,000 registered users (about 500 paying) and has raised roughly $380,000, including a $300,000 round in June 2026. Ashurov presented it to President Mirziyoyev on June 30. The figures are self-reported and have not been independently verified.

Wednesday, October 21, 2026 · Asia & Europe

Saudi Arabia

Asia · population ~37M

Politics & Government

Riyadh is leaning on allies as the Yemen war re-erupts. The GCC condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, and Egypt pledged “necessary” support, although its parliament delayed a vote on military backing. Separately, Turkiye is moving to ratify the Mecca joint defence pact with Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, and France’s president is touring the Gulf in search of an energy-for-security arrangement.

Economy & Business

The kingdom has postponed its Future Investment Initiative conference, the “Davos in the Desert” planned for October 26–29, until 2027 with no new date set. The event is a key showcase for investment in the push beyond oil, and government agencies and some Riyadh firms have told staff to work from home. Meanwhile, Gulf diesel is in demand: France is lining up more Gulf diesel as fuel prices climb.

Military & Security

Houthi drones and missiles have struck Saudi military, energy and civilian sites in recent weeks, including an attack on Riyadh’s King Khalid airport that killed 13 people. Media reports say Iran’s Revolutionary Guard aided the Houthis, which Tehran and the Houthis deny. The UK will deploy Typhoon jets to help defend the kingdom, and Saudi-led forces have used airstrikes to try to break the Houthi siege of Taiz.

Society & Culture

The humanitarian toll is spreading across the border in Yemen, where the UN refugee agency says more than 250,000 people have been displaced since fighting escalated. Inside the kingdom, Riyadh offices have asked staff to stay home, a sign of how the strikes are disrupting daily routines in the capital.

Tourism

Riyadh Season, the kingdom’s flagship entertainment festival, is scheduled to open today for a 10-week run. Its seventh edition has the theme “Big Time” and lists boxing, UFC bouts, the Six Kings Slam tennis tournament, concerts and the returning Boulevard World zone. The security situation is the open question, since the investment conference was postponed over the same attacks; check current travel advisories before planning a trip. This research did not find updated official visitor arrival figures for this week.

Site to visit: Hegra (AlUla) in the north-west is Saudi Arabia’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site, with Nabataean tombs carved into sandstone outcrops. It is best visited in the cooler months from roughly November to March, and tours of Hegra must be booked in advance. Verify current security and travel guidance before going.

🔥 Trending
GOAST FLOWER Saudi Music Community
Independent music label · Riyadh

GOAST FLOWER is a creative label founded by Saudi singer-songwriter Tamtam, and it is hosting a free Saudi Music Community night with Apple Music MENA at Personage in Riyadh’s JAX District on October 21. It is an artist-led effort to spotlight local acts. Note that this research found no hard streaming or social numbers for it, so treat it as an emerging scene to watch rather than a verified viral hit.

Russia

Europe · population ~144M (transcontinental; European portion listed here)

Politics & Government

US envoy Steve Witkoff has travelled to St Petersburg to meet Vladimir Putin after what Secretary of State Rubio called “positive” talks with Zelenskyy in Oslo, as Washington pushes to end a war now in its fifth year. On the Iran crisis, Foreign Minister Lavrov visited Tehran, and analysts ask whether Russia also gains from the US-Iran crisis. Trump meanwhile pressured Ukraine to cut a peace deal.

Economy & Business

Trump’s executive order paused sanctions on Russia and buyers of its oil and gas days before they were due to take effect, drawing bipartisan criticism; the waiver followed a deal for the US to buy Russian diesel to ease fuel prices. The Bank of Russia held its key rate at 14% in September, and its next decision is due on October 23. The bank expects inflation of 6–7% this year and has cut its 2026 growth forecast to 0–1%.

Military & Security

Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones at Russia, setting the St Petersburg oil terminal and the Ruchi oil depot ablaze and leaving about 20,000 people without power in the Leningrad region, according to the regional governor. Russia’s defence ministry said it neutralised 1,015 drones. Russian strikes continued on Ukraine, including an attack on a Kyiv high-rise, while NATO is sending additional patrol planes to the Black Sea and Finland is seeking one million land mines as it eyes the Russian threat.

Society & Culture

Russian social media has been dominated this year by memes: a Medialogia study of January to July found the “6-7” meme the most discussed, with more than 1.26 million mentions. Charts have also been shaped by short songs and AI-generated music, according to a Gazeta.ru review of the first half of 2026.

Tourism

Air travel is badly disrupted. Drone attacks forced Moscow and St Petersburg airports to halt flights overnight, with almost 200 flights delayed or cancelled on October 15–16, and restrictions returned the following night. Meduza reported that Agentstvo calculated the four airports were closed a combined 101 hours in the first 14 days of October, against 46 hours in all of September. Check airline notices and your government’s travel advice before booking.

Site to visit: The Historic Centre of Saint Petersburg and Related Groups of Monuments is a UNESCO World Heritage Site built around the imperial city’s canals, palaces and the Hermitage. It is usually visited in the long days of summer, and the metro makes the centre easy to reach from Pulkovo. Given the drone attacks and airport closures there this month, confirm current conditions before travel.

🔥 Trending

Meme / online culture · Russian social networks

Medialogia ranked “Cheremsha and Soviet aesthetics” the second most discussed meme in Russia from January to July 2026, with about 769,500 posts, behind the “6-7” gesture meme. It centres on AI-made videos in a Soviet style featuring a lion-hare character, an organic, user-driven trend. This research did not identify the original creator or city of origin, so the location is listed only as online; see also the AMIC report.

Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · Europe & Asia

Ukraine

Europe · population ~44M (pre-war estimate)

Politics & Government

Relations with Washington deteriorated sharply this week: on Monday Donald Trump said it may be time for Zelenskyy to get somebody else to negotiate, and Zelenskyy told US senators that arms deliveries paid for through the PURL programme were being held up. Trump also defended sanctions relief for Russian diesel exports, which Zelenskyy said would hurt Ukraine. Envoy Steve Witkoff is reportedly due in Norway for further talks. At home, Ukraine's EU integration chief criticised a proposal to choose the chief prosecutor through an open contest, as Brussels pushes for a merit-based process.

Economy & Business

Prime minister said Russian attacks have cost Ukrainian businesses $7.7 billion in 2026. Kyiv also plans to compensate damaged cargo ships after Russia rejected a Black Sea proposal. Meanwhile, the defence-tech sector keeps attracting foreign interest: the US ordered another batch of more than 1,300 FPV drones from Ukrainian maker F-Drones, according to AIN.

Military & Security

Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones at Russia overnight, one of its largest aerial attacks, setting the St Petersburg oil terminal and the Ruchi depot ablaze, Al Jazeera reported. Russia's own campaign continues: Zelenskyy said Moscow struck 28 critical energy facilities, leaving about 550,000 households without power. A Russian drone strike on a park also killed six people, including children, and Kyiv is asking why the Starshield system is failing.

Society & Culture

Kyiv faces a growing risk of a wastewater crisis this winter as attacks strain infrastructure. Commentators also warn about how Moscow exploits war fatigue in Ukrainian public opinion.

Science & Technology

Analysts are examining why Russia's strikes on Ukrainian data centres could backfire, while Kyiv's Starlink-enabled strike advantage is now at risk. Startups are also working on speeding up demining technology.

🔥 Trending
Zeely
AI marketing app · Ukraine

Zeely began as an app that lets small business owners create ads and launch campaigns without an agency or technical skills. The Ukrainian startup won first place at a Deel and J.P. Morgan competition in Paris, beating more than 50,000 applicants, and Deel is investing $1 million. It is now preparing Zeely 2.0, an AI marketing platform with an agentic layer coordinating the full marketing cycle, as described in an AIN interview.

Turkey

Asia · population ~86M

Politics & Government

Pressure on the opposition continued: police detained the opposition mayor of Çukurova and 43 others in a corruption probe, and the AKP took over another opposition-run Istanbul district. Four people were jailed in the probe into the Halk TV takeover. Prosecutors also sought up to 50 years for 22 former judges and prosecutors. On diplomacy, Ankara urged Washington to lift sanctions as F-35 talks continue.

Economy & Business

An investment fund scandal dominates: the head of the capital markets regulator resigned, authorities are investigating trading in 31 companies, and MSCI froze index changes and warned of a possible downgrade. Erdoğan has vowed no losses for the public. Foreign direct investment fell 35% to $6.85 billion in the first eight months, and 93,095 small businesses closed in nine months.

Military & Security

Turkey signed more than $500 million in defence contracts with Azerbaijan, even as a diplomatic spat shows differences between the two. Authorities detained 22 people in new raids on the Süleymanlılar religious community, and Erdoğan said Gülen-linked schools had been shut or taken over in over 50 countries.

Society & Culture

Istanbul was shut down by its heaviest rainfall since 1985: floodwater reached two hospitals, schools closed citywide and a 14-year-old was electrocuted; the toll later rose to 8 across Istanbul and Kocaeli. Separately, 12 migrants drowned in two incidents off Greece in Aegean crossings.

Tourism

Tourism is running slightly below last year: Türkiye had 34.83 million foreign visitors in January-August, down 1.84%, with Istanbul leading ahead of Antalya, per Hürriyet Daily News. Antalya received nearly 12.5 million tourists in nine months, down about 5.6% from a year earlier. The Istanbul floods this week disrupted city travel.

Site to visit: Ephesus, near Selçuk in İzmir province, is a UNESCO-listed ancient Greek and Roman city famed for its Library of Celsus and Great Theatre, and one of the best-preserved classical cities in the Mediterranean (see the UNESCO listing). Visit in spring or autumn to avoid summer heat and crowds; Selçuk is roughly an hour from İzmir by road or rail.

Science & Technology

Culture Minister Mehmet Nuri Ersoy announced what he called the world’s oldest shipwreck, found near Kekova Island off Antalya: storage jars about 4,700 years old, traced to workshops in the Jordan Valley. The dig was led by Hakan Öniz of Akdeniz University.

🔥 Trending

Music (AI-assisted vocals) · Türkiye

Nun Sultan has built a following through an AI-assisted vocal sound and a distinct style. Her breakout track “Durdur Beni” (Stop Me) has gained wide traction, her streaming numbers keep rising, and rapper Sagopa Kajmer has praised her as “heading toward the top.” A second album and a full remix are reportedly in development, according to Daily Sabah.

Monday, October 19, 2026 · Africa

Nigeria

Africa · population ~225M

Politics & Government

Positioning for the 2027 elections dominated the weekend. In Kaduna, Governor Uba Sani said he is targeting three million votes as top allies of Nasir El-Rufai defect to the APC, while Rabiu Kwankwaso told the opposition ADC that his camp will not step down for Atiku Abubakar. Regionally, Burkina Faso, Niger and Mali have resumed talks with ECOWAS after quitting the bloc.

Economy & Business

Headline inflation eased to 14.63% in September, though food inflation edged up to 19.59%. Central Bank Governor Olayemi Cardoso explained the reasons behind the MPC's interest rate cut and praised coordination with fiscal authorities. On fuel, Finance Minister Taiwo Oyedele insisted there will be no return to fuel subsidy or multiple exchange rates, and the IMF has warned against blanket petrol subsidies in favour of targeted support.

Military & Security

Gunmen killed two officers in an attack on a police station in Abia on October 17. In Kano, officials said troops killed two suspected kidnappers, rescued a victim and recovered ransom. Separately, the Army explained its arrest of an Amnesty International reporter.

Tourism

Cross River State has launched the 2026 edition of Carnival Calabar under the theme "Rethinking Our Collective Destiny", with the flagship week set for December 26–30. The state tourism bureau says the 2025 festivals drew about 248,000 visitors and injected over N17.4 billion into the local economy, with hotel occupancy peaking above 95% in late December.

Site to visit: The Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove in Osun State is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of the last remnants of primary high forest in southern Nigeria and a living shrine to the river goddess Osun, dotted with sculptures and shrines. It is most lively during the annual Osun-Osogbo festival each August, and it can be reached by road from Lagos or Ibadan.

Science & Technology

A Gombe community has been connected to a 1MW solar power plant after 17 years off the national grid, an example of the off-grid renewable projects that are filling gaps in the country's power network.

🔥 Trending
Ubokobong Bionic Arm
Prosthetics · Akwa Ibom State

After a fireworks accident on New Year's Day 2019 cost Ubokobong Amanam his fingers, he could not find a prosthetic that matched his skin tone. His brother John, a special-effects artist, built him a realistic hand, and the pair went on to found Immortal Cosmetic Art to make lifelike, functional myoelectric arms for other Black amputees. TIME has now named the device among its best inventions of 2026, and the company says clients in 56 countries use it.

South Africa

Africa · population ~61M

Politics & Government

With local government elections set for November 4, President Cyril Ramaphosa told reporters that political-related killings are a huge concern, after two ANC councillors were shot dead while canvassing in Umgababa, KwaZulu-Natal. Campaign rhetoric is sharpening, with Julius Malema taking a swipe at Helen Zille, and former president Thabo Mbeki is due to appear before the TRC Cases Inquiry on Monday.

Economy & Business

Fuel costs are the flashpoint: a group calling itself Concerned Citizens of South Africa began a national shutdown on October 19 demanding cuts to fuel prices and pump-price levies, with road closures reported in Johannesburg. Reports say R10 billion in fuel relief is on the cards, and commentators note that government taxes and levies take about R7,900 from every petrol fill-up.

Military & Security

Police in the Free State reported more than 1,000 arrests in a Safer Festive Season operation, while in the Eastern Cape Peruvian women were rescued from a suspected human trafficking ring. The Border Management Authority also launched a new digital security initiative.

Society & Culture

Health authorities confirmed four new Mpox cases, bringing the total to 26. The weather service said heatwave conditions are expected to persist until Monday in four provinces, and a veld fire warning was issued for the Free State.

Tourism

South Africa recorded more than one million international arrivals in August, up 7.4% year on year, taking January–August arrivals past 7.5 million. The United States was the largest overseas market, followed by the UK and Germany, and Tourism Minister Patricia de Lille said the focus must now be on converting demand into investment and jobs; UN Tourism ranked the country Africa's strongest-performing destination in the first half of 2026.

Site to visit: Robben Island off Cape Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site where Nelson Mandela spent 18 of his 27 years in prison, and tours are led by former political prisoners. Ferries leave from the Nelson Mandela Gateway at the V&A Waterfront, and tickets should be booked ahead because weather can cancel crossings.

🔥 Trending
Khayelitsha Cookies
Food & export · Khayelitsha, Cape Town

Khayelitsha Cookies began as a community baking initiative and was bought for R1 in 2013 by business partners Adri Williams and Eunice Nyobole, who inherited roughly R2 million in debt. The women-owned bakery now supplies British Airways and has secured more than R1.4 million in UK-linked deals, a rise from township bakery to inflight supplier.

Sunday, October 18, 2026 · Asia

Vietnam

Asia · population ~98M

Politics & Government

Vietnam's new leadership team is settling in after the 16th National Assembly elected Le Minh Hung as Prime Minister and approved the rest of the government for the 2026–2031 term, as VNA's roster of cabinet members shows. The Assembly's second session is scheduled for October, with the Standing Committee having added several draft laws and resolutions to the agenda. General Secretary and President To Lam continues routine diplomacy, including a congratulatory message to Germany on its unity day. Coverage of the very latest days was thin, so these items reflect the early-October picture.

Economy & Business

The economy is running hot: Xinhua-reported official data put third-quarter growth near 10 percent, while the World Bank forecasts 7.4 percent growth for 2026, citing manufacturing and high-tech exports. Private forecasters are more bullish, with Standard Chartered keeping a 9.5 percent full-year call on strong trade. Officials are aiming for average growth of at least 10 percent across 2026–2030. Forecasts differ widely, so treat any single figure with caution.

Tourism

Vietnam welcomed 17.7 million foreign visitors in nine months, about 71 percent of the 25 million annual target, with September arrivals of roughly 1.77 million the best for that month in five years. China and South Korea are the top source markets, with European arrivals growing strongly. Officials expect the October-to-March peak season to close the gap to the target.

Site to visit: Ha Long Bay is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Quang Ninh province, famed for nearly two thousand limestone islets rising from emerald water. Most visitors take a day trip or overnight cruise from Bai Chay or Tuan Chau, roughly a two-and-a-half-hour drive from Hanoi; the cooler, drier months from autumn to spring are generally the most comfortable.

🔥 Trending
VSTRA
Indie music · Hanoi

Hanoi singer-songwriter VSTRA has carved out a distinctive niche in V-pop through independent releases rather than major-label promotion. A VTV Tune Box profile noted that recent releases such as “Em Muon Duoc Bay” drew hundreds of thousands of views soon after release. The rise fits a wider shift in which cheap singles and EPs let newcomers test audiences before committing to albums, as Vietcetera's look at Vietnamese artists describes.
