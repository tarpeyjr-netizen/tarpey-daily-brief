Saturday, October 10, 2026 · South America

Brazil

South America · population ~215M

Politics & Government

Brazil heads into a presidential runoff on October 25 after Senator Flávio Bolsonaro finished first in the October 4 vote with 47.03% to President Lula’s 45.16%, upending polls that had Lula narrowly ahead. Al Jazeera reports that the Liberal Party (PL) won 121 of 513 lower-house seats against 70 for Lula’s Workers’ Party, and that Michelle and Carlos Bolsonaro won Senate seats. Flávio has pledged to seek an amnesty for his father Jair, who is serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup, and analysts cited there note that no first-round runner-up has won the presidency since 1989.

Economy & Business

Inflation re-accelerated in September, with the IPCA index rising 0.82% on the month and 4.58% over 12 months, above forecasts of 0.73% and 4.50%. Housing costs led the increase as residential electricity jumped nearly 8% after an Itaipu bonus credit ended, and airfares climbed almost 10%. Al Jazeera adds that the real and the stock market have risen on expectations of a more business-friendly government. Meanwhile the Senate will vote on October 14 on a constitutional amendment cutting the weekly work cap from 44 to 40 hours with pay unchanged; the lower house passed it in May.

Society & Culture

Voter fatigue was visible in the first round, with about 30 million Brazilians (21% of the electorate) not voting. Crime has dominated the campaign, and Lula has answered the right’s security message with a tough-on-crime slogan while pushing to end the six-day work week, a measure a Quaest poll found about 70% of Brazilians support. Lula’s team is also trying to mobilise non-voters, including through online outreach to young people and fan communities.

Tourism

Site to visit: Iguaçu National Park in Paraná state, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, protects one of the world’s largest systems of waterfalls on the border with Argentina, with hundreds of cascades dropping into a subtropical forest. The Devil’s Throat is the centrepiece, and the Brazilian side offers the broadest panoramic views. Fly into Foz do Iguaçu airport, which is close to the park, and check current ticket prices and opening hours before you go.

Science & Technology

Brazilian aerospace had a busy week: Embraer’s media centre lists the maiden flight of a Brazil-built Gripen E fighter on October 7 and the delivery of LATAM Airlines Brazil’s first E195-E2 jet on October 8, the first of 12 due to the carrier by the end of 2026.

🔥 Trending
“Brasil com S” by DJ M4IA
Music · Uberlândia, Minas Gerais

M4IA is a 31-year-old DJ and producer from Uberlândia who started with school parties and a web radio, and restarted his career in 2021 after his distributor shut down and removed his songs. He made “Brasil com S” in four days with AI tools as a fan response to France’s World Cup song, blending funk percussion with electronic textures, and Billboard Brasil reports more than 1 million TikTok videos and weeks at the top of YouTube Shorts in Brazil. He seeded it himself across multiple social profiles, and it grew into a dance trend and spawned versions for other national teams.

Friday, October 9, 2026 · Asia

India

Asia · population ~1.44B

Politics & Government

Opposition INDIA bloc MPs marched toward the Election Commission office in New Delhi this week, stopped by police barricades, demanding that Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign; the bloc also planned a “Save Democracy” rally in Jammu on October 7. The dispute centres on the Special Intensive Revision of voter rolls, which has drawn court challenges. Voting took place on October 6 in the Nagaon Lok Sabha by-election in Assam, and in Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay campaigned for a TVK candidate in a bypoll held the same day.

Economy & Business

The Reserve Bank of India has held its repo rate at 5.25% for a fourth straight meeting, keeping a neutral stance while the rupee weakened and West Asia tensions pushed up energy prices. Annual inflation reached 4.38% in June, above the RBI’s 4% target for the first time in 17 months, and the central bank has projected FY2026/27 growth in the high-6% range. Official October trade and inflation prints are still to come.

Military & Security

India summoned Pakistan’s envoy on October 4 over cross-border infiltration, in a fresh strain on relations that remain shaped by the aftermath of Operation Sindoor. Separately, Ukraine said it is ready to consider India’s ceasefire proposal, according to Foreign Minister Sybiha.

Tourism

The festival season is the main travel driver this month. Navratri runs from October 11 to 19, with Dussehra on October 20, and Garba and Dandiya nights fill public grounds in Gujarat. West Bengal has planned a global campaign to promote Durga Puja, with displays at landmarks abroad, and travel outlets report Kolkata airfares rising ahead of the festival.

Site to visit: Kolkata’s Durga Puja pandals, recognised by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage, are at their peak around October 19, with elaborate themed structures across the city. Go early in the morning or on a weekday to avoid the heaviest crowds; a festival travel guide lists top places to visit.

Science & Technology

ISRO’s year began badly: PSLV-C62 failed after an anomaly in the PS3 stage, the second PSLV failure in about a year. ISRO was expected to resume satellite launches by October, with the uncrewed Gaganyaan test flight targeted for the end of 2026.

🔥 Trending
“Maai” by Pawan Singh
Bhojpuri music · Bihar / Uttar Pradesh belt

A Bhojpuri-language song by singer Pawan Singh was the number-one trending video on YouTube India as of October 6, with about 3.2 million views. Regional-language music from the Hindi heartland continues to build large audiences on streaming platforms without Bollywood backing, driven by festival-season demand and sharing on local social networks.

Thursday, October 8, 2026 · North America

Mexico

North America · population ~130M

Politics & Government

President Claudia Sheinbaum signed a decree barring future presidents and governors from holding a second nationality, a rule that takes effect for the 2028 cycle. Her morning press conferences have also been dominated by the Ayotzinapa case, where she rejected claims that her government is building a new “historical truth”, and by reports of newborn deaths at IMSS facilities in Jalisco and Durango. Separately, Environment Minister Alicia Bárcena told lawmakers that “the conditions for proceeding with fracking do not exist” under current regulations.

Economy & Business

Mexico’s trade surplus with the United States is up more than 20% this year, with January–August exports to the U.S. of $419.34 billion against $263.47 billion in imports. Fixed capital investment grew 6% in July, the fastest pace in two years, according to Mexico News Daily. The peso has eased from its summer highs, trading around 17.9 per dollar, and a Citi poll of Mexico-based analysts expects it near 18 by year-end and 18.50 by the end of 2027. Nuevo León’s governor also announced US$600 million in data-center infrastructure investment, raising questions about grid capacity.

Military & Security

Mexico is asking Washington for evidence of an alleged late-September shooting from Tamaulipas toward U.S. Coast Guard personnel, after which about 500 U.S. troops were sent to the border. Sheinbaum said Mexican forces detected no shots fired from Mexican territory and has deployed her own forces under Operation Mirror. In Jalisco, authorities captured Iván Cazarín, the CJNG regional boss known as “El Tanque”, in Zapopan; he is linked to a large fuel-theft network spanning several states.

Society & Culture

Jalisco Governor Pablo Lemus announced a water-rationing plan for Guadalajara, blaming the aging Chapala supply system and illegal discharges into the Las Pintas canal. Another feature looks at U.S.-born children of deported Mexican parents, who are Mexican by birthright but often struggle to enroll in school.

Tourism

The Caribbean coast is under strain: Volaris will end Tulum flights as arrivals fall, partly because of sargassum and rising visitor costs. Nationally the picture is stronger; the government reported 51.1 million international travelers in the first half of 2026, up 7.7% on the year, and expects the 2026 total to top last year’s 48 million overnight tourists.

Site to visit: The Pre-Hispanic City of Teotihuacan, about 50 km northeast of Mexico City, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famed for the Pyramids of the Sun and Moon and the Avenue of the Dead. Go early on a weekday to beat the heat and crowds; buses run regularly from Mexico City’s Autobúses del Norte terminal.

Science & Technology

September 2026 passed with no Atlantic hurricanes hitting Mexico, an unusual outcome last seen in 1994. A magnitude 4.9 earthquake with an epicenter near El Arenal was felt early on October 7, with no damage or injuries reported.

🔥 Trending
Guadalajara’s water protests
Citizen movement · Guadalajara, Jalisco

Residents of Guadalajara’s metropolitan area have organized around a contamination and supply crisis tied to the Las Pintas canal and the aging Chapala system. Their demonstrations pushed the state government into announcing a rationing plan, though activists say rationing treats the symptom rather than the illegal discharges behind it. The story is a snapshot of how local, neighborhood-level organizing is setting the agenda in one of Mexico’s largest cities.

Wednesday, October 7, 2026 · Europe

Spain

Europe · population ~48M

Politics & Government

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez called a snap general election for November 29 after Congress rejected two government housing decrees on Friday. His minority coalition had already suffered several parliamentary defeats, and he told reporters he needs to renew people’s support. Opinion polls point to a conservative People’s Party coalition with the far-right Vox as the most likely winner, which would be the first far-right entry into central government since Franco. Spain has also gone four years without a new state budget, according to Funcas analysis reported by Infobae.

Economy & Business

The budget stalemate has not stopped public spending from growing: Funcas estimates administration spending is up 19% over the period, with budget modifications reaching €76.9 billion in 2025, or 16.8% of state spending. Tax revenue has grown faster still, at 24%. The wider economy remains resilient, and the government has held to a forecast of around 2.2–2.4% GDP growth for 2026, well ahead of the euro-area average. Housing costs are the main drag on households, with the Al Jazeera report noting that average rents have almost doubled over the past decade.

Military & Security

Migration pressure on the North African enclave of Ceuta remains a security flashpoint after tens of thousands of people crossed from Morocco in late July. Madrid has since added a maritime barrier and more than 500 extra police, bringing the force in Ceuta above 1,600. In August, Moroccan forces arrested nearly 300 migrants trying to reach the city. The opposition has accused the government of reacting too slowly, and Sánchez told Congress in September it “has nothing to hide” over its response.

Society & Culture

The housing crisis is driving street politics. More than 70,000 people marched in Madrid on Saturday, with anger sharpened by the televised eviction of an 87-year-old woman. Smaller September marches over the same eviction had already put the issue at the top of the agenda. Asking prices for homes have risen by about 90% over the decade, according to the same Al Jazeera report.

Tourism

Spain received 12.3 million international tourists in August, 9.2% more than a year earlier, according to INE’s Frontur survey. Over the first eight months of 2026 arrivals rose 5.4% to about 70.4 million, led by the United Kingdom with more than 13.9 million visitors, followed by France and Germany. The country is on track for around 100 million international tourists this year, which would beat last year’s record of 96.8 million.

Site to visit: The Sagrada Família in Barcelona is Gaudí’s unfinished basilica, and its Tower of Jesus Christ reached structural completion in February 2026, making it the world’s tallest church at 172.5 metres. Pope Leo XIV inaugurated the spire in June, on the centenary of Gaudí’s death. General admission costs roughly €15–20, tickets are best bought online in advance, and weekdays are less crowded.

🔥 Trending
Sindicato de Inquilinas (tenants’ union movement)
Cultural movement · Madrid and Catalonia

The Sindicato de Inquilinas grew out of neighbourhood organising, and it now backs rent-reduction demands and the idea of rent strikes. Tenant unions have signed up thousands of members in Madrid and Catalonia and have called national protests. This week its cause moved from the streets into national politics, as a 70,000-strong Madrid march preceded the housing-decree defeat and the snap election call. Crowd sizes are disputed: organisers and authorities have given very different counts at past marches.

Tuesday, October 6, 2026 · South America

Venezuela

South America · population ~29M

Politics & Government

Acting President Delcy Rodríguez’s government and an opposition delegation agreed to work towards reform of the Supreme Court, creating a seven-person committee to vet justice candidates and a Judicial Nominations Committee in the National Assembly. The same round of talks also moved to lift administrative sanctions on several media outlets, and Rodríguez asked lawmakers for a partial reform of the 2017 anti-hate law. Pressure for a vote is growing, with Washington’s top diplomat calling for elections in Venezuela “as soon as possible”, though no date has been set.

Economy & Business

Foreign energy firms including Repsol, Maurel & Prom and Hunt Oil gathered at an oil summit in Caracas this week, hoping to expand output that now stands near 1 million barrels a day, against a historical average of about 3 million. Repsol is targeting a 50 percent increase in its western fields, while Maurel & Prom expects 23,000 barrels a day by year-end. Chronic power cuts, which interrupted the summit itself, and political uncertainty remain the main hurdles. Broader analysis of the economy’s state of play points to renewed growth but inflation still near 400 percent.

Society & Culture

Supporters of opposition leader María Corina Machado rallied on Sunday to demand her safe return from exile and a presidential election; her team says she has been blocked from coming home seven times. Meanwhile, daily life is strained by electricity shortages, with reports of lights going out and eroding public trust in many states.

Tourism

Domestic air links remain the gateway to Venezuela’s best-known destinations. Flights to the Los Roques archipelago were disrupted after a June earthquake and were slated to resume from Maiquétía in late August, per a booking guide for Los Roques, while Canaima is currently served by a single airline on limited days. Travellers should plan carefully for delays and check current advisories before booking.

Site to visit: Los Roques National Park is a Caribbean coral archipelago known for white-sand cays and clear water, reached by a roughly 40–45 minute flight from Caracas. Round-trip fares are typically quoted at US$250–385 per person with a 10 kg baggage limit, according to flight listings.

🔥 Trending
Rawayana & Akapellah – “Veneka”
Music · Caracas

Caracas band Rawayana, which grew from the city’s independent scene, teamed with rapper Akapellah on “Veneka,” a song that won a Latin Grammy for Best Latin Electronic Music Performance. It is part of a wave of Venezuelan acts, alongside Danny Ocean and Elena Rose, that a recent roundup of Venezuelan artists credits with turning local culture into one of the most compelling sounds in Latin music this year.

Monday, October 5, 2026 · Africa

Egypt

Africa · population ~106M

Politics & Government

President Abdel-Fattah El-Sisi has opened preparations for Egypt Forum 2026, scheduled for 19–24 October, with a dedicated website inviting public participation. Meanwhile, parliament is weighing a one-year postponement of the new Criminal Procedure Law after heated committee debate, as the government says preparations are incomplete, according to Ahram Online. On the diplomatic front, leaders of Egypt, Sudan, Eritrea and Somalia issued a joint declaration at a summit in New Alamein rejecting recognition of “Somaliland” and condemning unilateral measures on the Nile. The government is also preparing an October conference to detail economic challenges and reforms.

Economy & Business

Egypt’s economy is mixed: reserves hit a record US$57.2 billion and urban inflation eased to 14.5% in August, but the pound has slipped to roughly 52 per US dollar. The IMF trimmed its 2026 growth forecast to 4.2%, blaming regional tensions and higher oil prices rather than domestic policy. The US$8 billion IMF programme expires on 15 December, putting pressure on the next review. Prime Minister Madbouly has said the government targets reducing public debt to 78% of GDP.

Tourism

The Grand Egyptian Museum, fully open since late 2025, continues to drive Cairo’s tourism momentum, with reports citing roughly 15,000 visitors a day and millions of visitors so far. October marks the start of the cooler sightseeing season, which runs through April and brings peak demand to the Giza Plateau and the Tutankhamun galleries. Advance timed bookings are advised.

Site to visit: The Grand Egyptian Museum beside the Giza Pyramids is among the largest archaeological museums in the world, housing the complete Tutankhamun collection and a colossal statue of Ramesses II. It is best visited between October and April, and booking a timed ticket online in advance avoids queues at the Tutankhamun galleries.

Science & Technology

Egypt is positioning itself as a clean-energy manufacturing hub. A government report says the country is expected to add about 2 GW of solar capacity in 2026 while pushing to localise equipment production. In the Suez Canal Economic Zone, ELITE Solar has commissioned a 3-GW panel and 2-GW cell complex, and the Suez Canal Authority has launched new tugboats built domestically as part of efforts to localise maritime industries.

🔥 Trending
“Jealousy” Egyptian Remix
Music · Egypt

A young Egyptian producer, Moamen Moaa, reworked a South African track into an Egyptian-flavoured remix that became the number one trend in Egypt on TikTok. Now available on Spotify and other platforms, the track spread through user videos rather than any major label push.
