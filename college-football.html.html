Primary Teams

Texas A&M Aggies No. 8 AP · SEC

Next: Sat, Sept. 5 vs. Missouri State · 7:00 p.m. ET · ESPN · Kyle Field, College Station
  • QB Marcel Reed picked up his fifth preseason honor of the summer, landing on the Manning Award watch list. The senior captain and 2025 Manning finalist carries 5,407 career passing yards, 41 touchdown passes and 1,085 rushing yards into the year. (12thMan.com, Aug. 25)
  • Three Aggies — Reed, WR Terry Bussey and RB Rueben Owens II — made the Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award preseason watch list, which recognizes the top Texas-tied offensive player. Owens led A&M with 639 rushing yards and five scores a year ago; Bussey was third in the SEC with 421 kick-return yards. (12thMan.com, Aug. 25)
  • Safety and captain Dalton Brooks was named to the Chuck Bednarik Award watch list after a 62-tackle, 6.5-TFL, four-sack season. (12thMan.com, Aug. 24)
  • The athletics department launched a long-form interview series, "Untold with Andrew Monaco," with Mike Elko as the debut guest, talking through his path to Aggieland and how he builds a program culture. Episodes run monthly. (12thMan.com, Aug. 25)
  • Following a Monday league call, the SEC issued a statement effectively barring athletes who have signed professional contracts from returning to college competition — "College athletes, not former professional athletes, have been at the heart of the Southeastern Conference." (12thMan.com, Aug. 25)
  • New offensive coordinator Holmon Wiggins came out of the first fall scrimmage praising a deep receiver room he compared to "the Avengers," while flagging penalties and execution as the fix-it list. Elko said the team "functioned operationally at a really really good level" but was inconsistent on both sides. (KXXV, Aug. 18)
  • A statue of R.C. Slocum, the winningest coach in program history, will be unveiled outside the north end of Kyle Field at 2 p.m. on Friday, Sept. 4, the day before the opener. (12thMan.com, Aug. 24)

Penn State Nittany Lions No. 18 AP · Big Ten

Next: Sat, Sept. 5 vs. Marshall · 3:30 p.m. ET · FS1 · Beaver Stadium, University Park
  • QB Rocco Becht was added to the Manning Award preseason watch list — his fourth preseason list of 2026 after the Maxwell, Unitas Golden Arm and Davey O'Brien. No Nittany Lion has won the Manning since it began in 2004. (Onward State, Aug. 24)
  • Becht, who started 39 straight games at Iowa State and threw for more than 9,200 yards and 64 touchdowns there, said the job in Happy Valley is different from joining a settled program: this roster has to "bring everything together" in camp. He is taking one online graduate class this fall. (StateCollege.com, Aug. 20)
  • Camp standouts, per the staff: LG Trevor Buhr (Iowa State transfer) drew "offensive MVP of camp" talk from OC Taylor Mouser, LB Tony Rojas has been a full participant coming off an October 2025 ACL injury, and RB Quinton Martin Jr. has added weight without losing versatility. (Sports Illustrated, Aug. 18)
  • Injury watch heading into the final week of camp: LB Alex Tatsch and S Jeremiah Cooper both opened camp limited but are tracking toward Week 1 availability, while TE Gabe Burkle has been limited with a knee issue. (On3 / Victory Bell Rings, Aug. 4)
  • Matt Campbell's first camp — the program's first new head coach since 2014 — runs through Aug. 31. Seven Iowa State transfers came with him, including RB Carson Hansen, TE Benjamin Brahmer and C Brock Riker, and the left tackle competition among Malachi Goodman, Owen Aliciene and Garrett Sexton is still the biggest unresolved job. (Victory Bell Rings, Aug. 4)

Villanova Wildcats FCS · Patriot League

Next: Fri, Aug. 28 vs. William & Mary · 6:00 p.m. ET · ESPN+ · Villanova Stadium
  • The Patriot League's Council of Presidents executive committee denied Villanova's appeal on QB Pat McQuaide's eligibility, McQuaide's attorney Richard Hamilton Jr. confirmed. The league is leaning on its four-seasons-of-competition rule and has warned that games could be forfeited if he plays. (Philadelphia Inquirer, Aug. 22)
  • The denial runs against an Aug. 1 preliminary injunction from Judge Timothy McCormick of the Cuyahoga County Court of Common Pleas, which restored McQuaide's eligibility on the finding that the NCAA's waiver denial ignored COVID-era disruption to his recruiting and development. A motion for clarification is now back before the court. (The Villanovan / VU Hoops, Aug. 1–11)
  • With the opener two days out, the quarterback room is senior Robert Russo and freshman Nolan DiLucia, who took the majority of first-team reps through spring and summer. McQuaide threw for 3,123 yards and 25 touchdowns last season and led 'Nova to the FCS semifinals. (Philadelphia Inquirer, Aug. 22)
  • Friday is the first Patriot League football game in program history. Villanova officially joined for football on July 1 after 19 seasons in CAA Football, following William & Mary and Richmond into the league — which makes the opener a conference game against a familiar old rival. (Villanova.com, July 1)
  • The returning core is heavy on the ground game: RB Ja'briel Mace (946 yards, 11 TDs) and Isaiah Ragland (755 yards) are both back, along with WR Braden Reed, LBs Turner Inge and Omari Bursey, and DE Capri Martin. The 2025 defense finished 19th nationally at 20.9 points allowed but loses several secondary starters. (HERO Sports FCS preview, May 18)
  • The athletic department rolled out new gameday activities and fan experiences for the first Patriot League season, including changes to the pregame footprint at Villanova Stadium. (Villanova.com, Aug. 17)
